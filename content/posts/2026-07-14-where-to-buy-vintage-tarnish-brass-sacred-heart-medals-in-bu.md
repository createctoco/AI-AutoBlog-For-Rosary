## Sourcing Vintage Tarnish Finish Brass Sacred Heart Medals: A Buyer’s Guide for Bulk Supply

For church supply buyers, importers, and parish procurement officers, selecting a devotional medal involves balancing liturgical appropriateness with practical supply chain constraints. The vintage tarnish finish brass Sacred Heart of Jesus medal offers a distinct aesthetic that appeals to traditional sensibilities, but sourcing it in bulk requires careful attention to material, finish consistency, and order quantities. This article addresses the key decisions you will face when purchasing these medals for congregations, gift shops, or sacramental programs.

### Understanding the Vintage Tarnish Finish: Material and Aesthetic Tradeoffs

A “vintage tarnish finish” on a brass medal is not a defect but a deliberate surface treatment that mimics the patina of aged metal. This finish is often achieved through chemical oxidation or controlled application of darkening agents, creating a muted, antique appearance that contrasts with bright polished brass. For Catholic devotional use, this finish can evoke a sense of reverence and timelessness, complementing traditional church interiors and older statuary.

When sourcing such medals, confirm with us whether the tarnish is a sealed lacquer finish or an unsealed oxidation. Sealed finishes resist further darkening from handling and moisture, while unsealed finishes may continue to patina over time, which can be desirable or problematic depending on your use case. Brass itself is a durable alloy, but the tarnish layer can be susceptible to scratching if handled carelessly during packaging or installation.

We offer these medals in bulk, but the exact dimensions, weight, and finish specifications vary by item. Before placing an order, request a sample or confirm the specific finish depth and color tone. For OEM orders, where a church name or parish name is customized on tags or packaging, the finish must remain consistent across the production run, so we recommend approving a finished sample before full production begins.

### Bulk Order Quantities: MOQ Considerations for Wholesale Buyers

Bulk buying for church events or retail resale requires understanding minimum order quantities (MOQs). For in-stock vintage tarnish finish brass Sacred Heart medals, our MOQ is **12 pieces**. This low threshold is suitable for small parishes testing a new item for a gift shop or for a limited sacramental event such as a First Communion class.

For OEM orders that require a custom logo, church name, or parish name on the medal’s tag or packaging, the MOQ rises to **1200 pieces**. This higher volume reflects the setup and tooling involved in imprinting or embossing custom text. If your parish needs fewer than 1200 custom pieces, consider whether the in-stock option with generic packaging meets your needs, or combine your order with another diocese or organization to reach the OEM threshold.

Keep in mind that custom packaging—such as prayer cards with your parish name—may also be eligible under the OEM MOQ, but confirm with us what specific elements can be customized. Do not assume that the medal itself can be engraved; customization is limited to eligible tags or packaging unless otherwise stated.

### Finish Consistency and Quality Checks for Bulk Orders

When ordering 1200 or more custom medals, finish consistency becomes critical. Brass medals from a single production run may show slight variations in tarnish depth due to the chemical process. To mitigate this, we recommend asking for a “first article” or pre-production sample before the full batch is manufactured. This sample should represent the exact finish you will receive in bulk.

Check for the following during your evaluation:

- **Uniformity of color**: The tarnish should not appear blotchy or streaky across the medal’s surface.
- **Edge definition**: The Sacred Heart image (typically showing Jesus with a radiant heart) should be crisp, not blurred by excess tarnish.
- **Loop or bail integrity**: If the medal is meant to be worn, the attachment loop must be securely soldered. A loose loop can cause loss of the medal during use.
- **Packaging quality**: For OEM orders, confirm that the custom tags or packaging are free of smudges or alignment errors.

If you plan to use the medal in a rosary, note that our cord rosaries offer Peruvian knots or flat knots, but you must confirm the selected item before ordering. Not all cord types are compatible with every medal loop size.
