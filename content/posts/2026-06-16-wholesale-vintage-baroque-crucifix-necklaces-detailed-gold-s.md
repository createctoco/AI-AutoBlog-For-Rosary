## Vintage Baroque Detailed Gold Crucifix Necklace — Wholesale Insights from a Catholic Factory

You’d think a crucifix is just a crucifix. But then someone asks for a *baroque* style. Not the music. The design. Ornate, detailed, heavy-looking gold work. We get that request a lot from church gift shops and religious goods distributors.

So here’s the thing about wholesale vintage baroque gold crucifixes. They’re not all made the same. Some are stamped tin with gold paint. Some are cast metal with real gold plating. We do the latter. Our factory has been making these for years, mostly for Catholic parishes in the US, Philippines, and parts of Europe. Let’s talk about what you’re actually buying when you order from us.

### What Makes a Baroque Crucifix “Baroque”?

Honestly, it’s the fussiness. The extra curls, the filigree, the exaggerated corpus. A baroque crucifix doesn’t whisper — it shouts. We’re talking about intricate detailing on the cross arms, maybe a decorative I.N.R.I. plaque, and a corpus that looks sculpted, not stamped flat.

We make ours in solid brass or zinc alloy, then plate it with real gold. Not the cheap stuff that peels off after a month. The finish is thick enough to last through handling, blessing, and maybe a few drops of holy water. The chain? We usually pair it with a heavy curb or rolo chain — nothing flimsy.

If you’re a wholesaler, you’ve probably seen the cheap Chinese knockoffs. Thin metal, hollow back, gold paint that rubs off on your fingers. That’s not what we ship. Our crucifixes have weight. You pick one up, you feel it.

### Sizes, Chains, and Customization — What We Actually Do

Most of our wholesale buyers ask for 2-inch to 3-inch crucifixes. That’s the sweet spot for a necklace. Big enough to see the detail, small enough to wear under a shirt.

We can do different chain lengths — 18 inches, 24 inches, 30 inches. Depends on your market. Some parishes want shorter chains for women. Some want longer ones for men or for wearing over vestments.

Custom logo? Yes. We can put your church name, parish name, or distributor brand on the packaging or on a small metal tag attached to the chain. OEM orders start at 1200 pieces. For our in-stock items, you can start as low as 12 pieces. Buy more, pay less. That’s the simple math.

We also do different finishes. Gold is the most popular for baroque styles. But we’ve done antique gold (darker, aged look), silver, and even rose gold for some special orders. The antique gold finish actually works better for baroque designs — it makes the details pop.
