### Why Custom Engraving Matters for Confirmation

Confirmation is the moment a young Catholic chooses their saint name. That name becomes part of their spiritual identity. A cross pendant with that name engraved on it? That’s not just jewelry. It’s a reminder of a promise.

We’ve had churches order 500 pendants, each with a different saint name. Sounds like a nightmare, right? Not for us. Our production team handles custom engraving in-house. We don’t outsource it. That means we control the quality and the turnaround time.

The pendants themselves are solid. Not plated. Not stamped. We use real metal — brass or stainless steel, depending on the order. And the engraving is deep, not surface-level. You can feel it with your finger.

One thing we learned early on: don’t use laser engraving on thin pendants. It burns through. We use rotary engraving instead. Deeper, cleaner, and it doesn’t fade.

### Size Matters – Pick the Right One

We get asked about pendant sizes a lot. For confirmation gifts, we usually recommend 18mm to 25mm. Too small and the name is hard to read. Too big and it looks like a costume piece.

Our most popular size? 20mm. That’s about the size of a dime. Big enough to engrave “St. Michael” or “St. Teresa” clearly. Small enough to wear daily.

We also offer different chain lengths. 18 inches for teens. 20 inches for adults. Some parishes prefer a simple cord instead of a chain — we do that too.
