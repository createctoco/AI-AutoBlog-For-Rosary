## Material and Design Highlights

The base metal is stainless steel, which matters more than the finish in a wholesale context. Stainless steel resists tarnishing and corrosion, so pendants hold their appearance through handling, storage and everyday wear. For you, that translates into fewer returns and fewer complaints from end consumers who expect a charm to still look right after a few months.

Gold plating sits on top of that base, applied to a smooth refined surface. The oval form gives the piece a defined edge and a clean face for the stone setting. Colorful rhinestones are arranged across the face in a pattern that leans vintage — think old-world glamour rather than high-contrast modern minimalism. It's a look that pairs easily with gold-tone chains, cord, ribbon or leather, which is part of why the set is flexible for finished-piece assembly.

Durability is the practical story here. Because the substrate is stainless steel rather than a softer alloy, these pendants tolerate the kind of handling that bulk inventory goes through — packing, unpacking, display touching, reshipping. That's a real consideration if you're distributing to Amazon sellers or shipping to retail displays where product gets handled constantly.

Mecrt maintains consistent sizing and finish across each set, which simplifies inventory management. Anyone who has received a reorder that didn't quite match the first batch knows why that matters.

## What to Verify Before Ordering

Specifications aren't published in a standard spec sheet for this listing, so a few points deserve confirmation before you commit to a bulk run. Ask for the exact pendant dimensions and weight, the plating thickness or finish description, and the stone-setting method. If your channel requires it, request confirmation on nickel content and any relevant material documentation.

Sampling is the second step. Ordering a small quantity at the 12-piece in-stock MOQ gives you physical pieces to evaluate — plating appearance under your own lighting, stone adhesion, edges, and how the pendant hangs on the chain or cord you plan to pair it with. Photograph them next to your existing product line; that's usually where you discover whether the gold tone matches what your buyers expect.

Also clarify what's included in the set. "Charm set" can mean a multi-piece assortment or a single design sold in quantity, and the packaging format affects your landed cost and handling time. Ask how the pieces arrive — individually bagged, bulk-packed, or on a display card — and whether custom packaging is available at the OEM threshold.

Finally, confirm lead time and shipping terms in writing. Neither is stated in the source material, so treat both as order-specific and get them confirmed before you finalize quantities.
