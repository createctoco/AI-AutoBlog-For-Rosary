## Materials and Craftsmanship

Polished stainless steel is the material here, and the practical reason matters more than the technical one. It resists tarnish, corrosion, and everyday wear, which means the shine you see in a sample holds up through repeated handling on your assembly line — and later, through daily wear by the end customer. That combination is what keeps a bulk order from turning into a returns problem six months down the road.

The cross motif is embossed, not printed. Raised relief won't rub off or fade with contact, and it gives the pendant a tactile quality that photographs well for product listings. The circular shape provides a flat, balanced surface, which is useful if you're mounting pendants on display cards or using them where a lopsided silhouette would look off.

Uniformity is the other half of the story. Consistent dimensions and pre-drilled holes mean you can plan labor around the component rather than around its inconsistencies — no reaming holes to fit a jump ring, no returning a partial batch because half the pieces sit at an odd angle.
