## What to Verify Before Ordering

Treat the specification sheet as a conversation, not a download. Dimensions and unit weight aren't published on the listing, so ask for both — they drive your landed cost calculation and your packaging choice. Request a physical sample before committing to a bulk run, especially if the pendant is destined for a retail display where customers handle it first.

Then work through the practical items:

- **Attachment method.** Whether the piece arrives pre-drilled, fitted with a bail, or plain varies by item. Confirm it matches how your customers will actually string or hang it.
- **Packaging spec.** If you're supplying parish programmes, ask how units are bagged or bulked. Loose count versus individually wrapped changes your labour at the receiving end.
- **Batch consistency.** Ask what variation to expect on tone and relief depth across a single run, and how the supplier handles that on reorders.
- **Documentation.** If your market requires import or compliance paperwork, ask what's available rather than assuming.
- **Lead time.** Not published, so confirm against your delivery window before you set a launch date.

Answer those five and most of the risk is gone. Skip them and you'll find out at customs or at the counter.
