## Materials and Craftsmanship

The tags are woven from durable polyester thread rather than printed. That distinction matters more than it sounds. Woven construction produces a clean, raised finish where logos, text, and patterns read sharply, and the detail doesn't break down the way surface printing can.

Softness is the other half of the story. A shoulder tag sits against skin, typically near the neckline or shoulder seam, so a scratchy label turns into a complaint you hear about from end users instead of a defect you catch in QC. Polyester thread gives a soft, non-irritating hand feel while still holding crisp definition on shirts, jackets, and outerwear.

Wondering how it survives a commercial laundry cycle? That's the practical test for institutional buyers, and it's where the material earns its place. The woven build resists fading and fraying through repeated laundering, which supports compliance with commercial laundry standards — useful if you're supplying uniforms or garments that go through industrial wash programs rather than a home machine.

Customization runs several layers deep. Buyers can specify colors, sizes, and care symbols as part of the woven design rather than as an afterthought. Thread color choice changes how the finished tag reads against the garment, so it's worth pinning that down before artwork is finalized. Mecrt also supports refinement of tag dimensions and edge finishing during development, which is where a lot of the practical comfort and durability decisions actually get made.

## Bulk Ordering Details

Custom work follows a familiar OEM/ODM path: you submit artwork, the team works through the details with you, and production follows. Mecrt's quotations are built around three variables — design complexity, order quantity, and thread color specifications. There's no published price list, and that's normal for woven labels. A two-color text tag and a multi-color emblem with fine detail aren't the same job.

For stock designs, the entry point is low at 12 pieces, which makes trial orders and sample batches realistic. Custom and OEM programs start at 1,200 pieces. Stock support is part of the offer, and it's aimed at repeat buyers: holding consistent inventory shortens lead times on reorders instead of restarting the production cycle each time.

Before you place a bulk order, get these confirmed in writing:

- **Finished dimensions.** A tag that's a few millimeters off can sit wrong inside the seam.
- **Edge finishing.** Options vary by item, so contact us to confirm what's available for your specification.
- **Thread colors** matched against your brand palette, ideally with physical references rather than screen colors.
- **Care symbols**, which need to match what the garment can actually withstand.
- **Artwork requirements.** Ask which file formats the design team accepts before you invest time in cleanup.
- **A pre-production sample** for any custom run.

Pricing moves with quantity, so it's worth requesting quotes at two or three volume breaks rather than a single figure. That gives you something to plan against. And if you expect repeat orders, ask up front whether your chosen specification can be held in stock. Mecrt runs 24/7 online customer service, so time zones are rarely a blocker on scheduling.
