## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk order, run through the specification list. Alloy composition, wood type and finish aren't detailed on the product page, so ask for the material spec sheet if those details matter for your market or your customers' expectations.

Confirm the chain length options and how bead arrangement changes across variants. If you're producing multiple SKUs, get the differences in writing so your retail listings match what actually ships.

Sampling is the other big one. Handmade items vary, and a sample tells you far more about weight, finish and wood tone than any photo will. Ask what the sample timeline looks like and whether sample costs are credited against a bulk order.

Then there's documentation. If your market requires import or compliance paperwork, ask early — requirements vary by destination and by item, and it's better to know before production than after. Lead times also vary by item and order size, so confirm them against your own delivery schedule.

Finally, nail down packaging, labeling and any branding you need applied. These details affect both unit cost and production time.
