## Materials and Craftsmanship

Zinc alloy is a sensible choice at this price tier. It casts cleanly, takes a polish well, and holds detail better than cheaper die-cast alternatives — which is why it's the default for costume jewelry components that need to look consistent across thousands of units. The rhinestones are set into the pendant and charm components with attention to detail, giving the piece a subtle brilliance rather than a flashy one.

The unisex styling deserves a second look, because it's harder to pull off than it sounds. Most rhinestone accessories lean heavily feminine in their proportions and setting patterns. This one stays restrained — the kind of piece that works on a simple chain for everyday wear or layered with heavier links for a more styled look. That range is what makes it sellable to a broad demographic.

Mecrt runs a production operation of over 160 artisan craftsmen with monthly output above 100,000 pieces, and the company's handmade rosary lines retain traditional hand-knotting techniques.
