## Checklist Before You Commit

Run through this before you sign off on a bulk order.

1. **Request a physical sample.** Photographs won't tell you how the wood feels in hand or how prominent the scent is at arm's length.
2. **Test the scent over time.** Closed bag, open air, and a few weeks of ordinary handling. Note whether it fades evenly or drops off sharply.
3. **Inspect the stringing.** Check knot tension, cord thickness at the centerpiece, and whether the beads slide freely without gapping.
4. **Examine the crucifix.** Look at edge finishing, attachment points and whether the plating or coating is even on both faces.
5. **Compare color across beads in one piece, then across several pieces.** Dye lot variance shows up at the batch level, not on a single sample.
6. **Confirm packaging specification in writing.** Bag type, header card, barcode placement — these details get lost in verbal agreements.
7. **Get MOQ, tier pricing and lead time in the quotation.** Not in a chat message, not inferred from a catalog page.

If any of those come back unclear, ask again. A supplier who answers specification questions precisely is a supplier you can reorder from without inspecting every shipment.
