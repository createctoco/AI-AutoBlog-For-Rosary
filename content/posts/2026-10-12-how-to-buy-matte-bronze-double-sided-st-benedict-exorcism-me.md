A matte bronze double-sided St Benedict exorcism medal is a small Catholic devotional piece. For a wholesale buyer, the sourcing decision is less about the medal image alone and more about finish consistency, attachment method, packaging, and the order path that fits your quantity. The following sections outline what to confirm before you place a bulk order.

## What a Double-Sided St Benedict Exorcism Medal Is—and Why the Finish Matters in Bulk

The St Benedict medal is a Catholic sacramental. The front typically shows St Benedict holding a cross and a book, while the back carries a cross and the letters associated with the Benedictine blessing. A double-sided medal presents devotional content on both faces, so the reverse is not left blank. Buyers often call it an “exorcism medal” because the medal’s traditional blessing and inscriptions are associated with protection and the invocation of Christ’s power against evil.

For a wholesale program, double-sided construction gives each piece a fuller visual identity. It also means two faces must be checked for legibility, alignment, and consistent finish. A matte bronze finish has a subdued, non-reflective surface that can read as traditional in a church setting and understated in a gift shop display. If your brief specifies matte bronze, confirm that the selected item is available in that finish. Finish names and surface treatments vary by item, so the sample you approve should be the standard for the bulk order.

## Specification Checks Before You Request a Wholesale Quote

Medals are often described by a short product name, but the physical specification can vary. Before comparing suppliers, write down the details that matter to your end users. These include the medal’s overall size, the attachment point, and the packaging format. If any of these are missing from a listing, ask for confirmation rather than assuming the specification.

### Size, Attachment, and Packaging: Confirm These Per Item

Size affects how the medal wears and how it is perceived. A smaller medal may suit a child’s First Communion gift, while a larger medal may be chosen for a wall plaque or a display. Because size availability varies by item, confirm the exact size you need for the selected medal.

Attachment is another variable. Some medals are designed with a jump ring or loop for a chain, cord, or ribbon. Others may be supplied as a pendant only, with the chain or cord ordered separately. Confirm whether the attachment is fixed, open, or compatible with the cord you plan to include.

Packaging choices also vary. A simple poly bag, a printed card, or a small pouch each creates a different presentation and handling cost. For parish orders, a card with a prayer or a parish name can be useful. For retail, a pouch or display card may be more appropriate. Check what packaging is available for the specific medal item.

Finally, inspect a pre-production sample for the details that matter in bulk: edge smoothness, lettering clarity, and consistency between the front and back. A matte finish can show handling marks, so ask how the pieces are protected before packing.

## MOQ and Customization: Two Order Paths for Buyers

Wholesale buyers usually fall into two groups: those who need a small quantity to test a design or supply a specific event, and those who need a custom program with parish or ministry branding. The order path differs for each.

For in-stock items, the MOQ is 12 pieces. This path suits buyers who need a modest quantity for a parish store, a retreat, or a small event. It also allows you to order a sample quantity to review the finish and attachment before committing to a larger program.

For OEM or custom-logo orders, the MOQ is 1200 pieces. On eligible tags or packaging, a logo, church name, or parish name can be customized. This is the appropriate path when you want the medal to carry a consistent identity for a diocese, parish, school, or ministry. Confirm which tags or packaging formats are eligible for customization, because not every item or packaging type may qualify.

The tradeoff is straightforward. The in-stock path gives you a lower entry quantity and faster access to a standard item. The OEM path requires a higher quantity but allows the order to carry your organization’s name. Decide which matters more for your channel. If you need both a small test order and a custom program, place the small order first to approve the physical item, then plan the OEM order with the approved specification.
