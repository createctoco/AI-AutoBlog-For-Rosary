## Design and Craft Details

The figurine is made from zinc alloy through precision die-casting. That's a meaningful detail for bulk buyers, because die-casting delivers uniformity across a production run. When you're ordering in volume, you don't want one piece to feel noticeably heavier or rougher than the next. The process here is designed to keep weight and texture consistent from unit to unit.

The surface treatment is where the product earns its "vintage" label. A distressed finish is cast into the metal, and a subtle patina enhances the aged look. Because the finish is part of the metal's surface character rather than a separate coating, it's better suited to items that get handled regularly. That's a practical point, since desk and dashboard ornaments tend to see a lot of contact. The zinc alloy construction gives the piece a solid, substantial feel and resists everyday wear.
