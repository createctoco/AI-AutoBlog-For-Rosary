## Checklist Before You Commit

Before you place a bulk order, pin down a few things directly with the supplier.

Samples first. No product page replaces holding the actual pendant — checking the weight, the engraving sharpness, and the finish in daylight. A 15mm piece photographs differently than it feels. Request one before you scale up.

Verify dimensions and per-unit weight for your own planning. You know your display cards, boxes, and shipping cost model. Confirm the 15x15mm spec matches what your customers expect and that the piece fits whatever retail packaging you intend to use.

Ask about finish variations. The standard product carries a polished, tarnish-resistant surface. If your line includes both polished and matte options, inquire whether that's available for this pendant.

Clarify packaging. Bulk poly bags work for some channels; retail-ready cards work for others. If your buyers are parish bookstores, a devotional card with the Saint Benedict prayer might move the product faster than a loose coin pendant. Mecrt supports custom packaging — use that capability deliberately.

Confirm how the MOQ applies in practice. Is the 12-piece in-stock minimum per design or per order? For custom engraving work, does the 1,200-piece threshold flex depending on the modification? These numbers change the economics of your order, so get them in writing before you issue a purchase order.

Finally, ask for lead times. Capacity figures tell you the supplier can produce at scale, but they don't tell you when your specific order ships. Your seasonal ordering calendar needs a concrete date, not an estimate formed from a website.
