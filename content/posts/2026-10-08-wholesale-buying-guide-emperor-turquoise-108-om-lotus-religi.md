## Checklist Before You Commit

Run through this before the purchase order goes out.

**Sample first, always.** Count the beads yourself. 108 is easy to skim past visually and hard to argue about later.

**Judge stone tone in person.** Ask what tolerance is normal across a bulk run so your retail description matches what actually arrives.

**Inspect the pendant carving** under angled light. Lotus detail is where hand-finishing shows, and it's the focal point of the piece.

**Confirm cord and tassel construction** in writing. Both are failure points on a strand worn daily.

**Get the spec sheet in writing** — bead diameter, strand length, pendant dimensions. Don't rely on the listing.

**Match the MOQ to the configuration.** 12 pieces in-stock, 1200 for custom. Mixed expectations here cause most of the friction on first orders.

**For private labeling, request a pre-production sample.** Confirm how the logo is applied and how it holds up on a curved bead surface.

**Ask about packaging options** and whether gift-ready presentation is available. This varies by order, so confirm rather than assume.

**Ask how quality is controlled across bulk runs.** Consistency is the whole reason to buy wholesale rather than piece by piece.
