## Checklist Before You Commit

Sample first. Order a single piece, get it in hand, and check the bead finish, the weight of the pendant and how the strand hangs. Photos are useful, but hematite luster is a tactile judgment — you want to see it under your own store lighting before committing to 1,200 units.

Confirm the specs you'll be held to. Bead size, material, cord construction, pendant dimensions, packaging format. Anything not stated in the product listing should be written into your quotation, so there's no gap between what you expected and what ships.

Ask about unit weight and packaging dimensions. Repacking or rerouting at the warehouse eats your margin, and a rosary sold as a gift lives or dies on whether the box feels appropriate for the price point.

Check your own market requirements. Labeling, import documentation, any compliance rules that apply to religious goods in your jurisdiction — that's your side of the deal, but it shapes what you order and how it's packed.

Then decide target quantities per SKU before you ask for pricing. A quote for 12 pieces and a quote for 12,000 pieces are different conversations, and arriving with a number keeps the exchange short.
