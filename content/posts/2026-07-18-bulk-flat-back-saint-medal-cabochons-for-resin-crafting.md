## Evaluating Flat Back Saint Medal Cabochons for Resin Craft Production

Church goods buyers and craft resellers sourcing components for devotional items face a specific challenge: finding flat back saint medal cabochons that are both theologically appropriate and practical for resin embedding. Unlike finished rosaries or medals, cabochons serve as raw material for custom altar pieces, prayer cards, memorial keepsakes, and group craft projects. This article focuses on the procurement decisions involved in selecting such cabochons for wholesale or bulk church use.

### Matching Cabochon Design to Intended Use

The first decision point is whether the cabochon will be used in a finished product that remains in the parish or is sold to individuals. For parish craft groups, resin-embedded saint medals are often made for First Communion classes, vacation Bible school take-home projects, or senior ministry gatherings. In these settings, the cabochon should feature a clear, recognizable image of a patron saint, the Blessed Virgin, or the Sacred Heart. Avoid generic or ambiguous iconography that may not align with Catholic devotional expectations.

For wholesale buyers importing cabochons for resale, consider the end user’s expectation of quality. Flat back cabochons intended for resin embedding must have a smooth, uniform back surface that adheres well without air bubbles. The front should be durable enough to withstand resin curing without fading or scratching. Because we do not specify exact material compositions for cabochons on this site, you should confirm with your supplier whether the cabochon face is printed, enameled, or metal-stamped. Each method affects adhesion and longevity.

### Sourcing Considerations for Bulk Orders

When ordering flat back saint medal cabochons in bulk, minimum order quantities (MOQs) are a critical factor. For in-stock items, the MOQ is 12 pieces, which suits small parish groups or test runs. For OEM or custom-logo cabochons—where you may want a specific saint image, parish name, or event date on the back—the MOQ is 1200 pieces. This higher threshold is typical for diocesan-wide projects, large retreats, or annual memorial distributions.

If you are considering combining cabochons with other components such as cord rosaries or wooden beads, note that our rosary offerings include natural solid wood beads (hand polished) and cord rosaries with either Peruvian knots or flat knots. These could complement a resin craft kit. For example, a memorial craft kit might include a flat back saint cabochon, a 6mm or 8mm wooden bead strand, and a crucifix. However, cabochon availability in specific bead sizes—6 mm, 8 mm, 10 mm, and 12 mm—varies by item, so confirm dimensions before ordering.

### Practical Checks Before Placing an Order

Before committing to a large cabochon order, verify three things with your supplier:

1. **Back surface flatness** – Even slight convexity can cause resin to pool unevenly. Ask for a sample or a dimensional drawing.
2. **Image permanence** – Resin can be chemically reactive. Confirm that the cabochon’s image will not bleed, peel, or dissolve during curing.
3. **Compatibility with your resin type** – Epoxy, polyester, and UV resins have different shrinkage and heat profiles. A cabochon that works with one may fail with another.

For OEM orders (1200+ pieces), you can customize the packaging or a small tag with your church name or logo. This is useful for parish-branded craft kits or gift shop items. The cabochon itself may also accept a printed logo on the reverse side, but this depends on the manufacturing method. Confirm this with us before finalizing artwork.
