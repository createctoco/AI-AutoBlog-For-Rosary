## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk run, a handful of details are worth pinning down in writing. These aren't standard across every order, so treat them as questions rather than assumptions.

**Material and finish.** Which wood species, and is it sealed or left natural? Both affect how the piece ages and how well engraving reads. Confirm the corpus material and finish as well — the appearance of the metal or wood figure matters to the customer holding it.

**Hardware.** Ask about the keyring's construction and how the crucifix attaches. This is the failure point on any keychain, and it's the first thing a retail customer complains about.

**Engraving spec.** Artwork format, engraving area, and whether a proof is provided before production. Get the proof in writing.

**Packaging.** Unit packaging type, whether retail labeling or barcodes can be applied, and units per carton. Carton configuration drives your landed cost more than most buyers expect.

**Commercials.** MOQ by configuration, price breaks at volume, production lead time, and sampling terms. Mecrt quotes wholesale pricing against your actual volume requirements, so give real numbers rather than a range — you'll get a more useful answer.

**Quality control.** Ask what the inspection process covers and request a pre-shipment sample or photo report. On a natural-material product, this is where surprises live.

A physical sample before a large order isn't optional here. Wood tone, carving depth, and keyring feel don't translate through a spec sheet.
