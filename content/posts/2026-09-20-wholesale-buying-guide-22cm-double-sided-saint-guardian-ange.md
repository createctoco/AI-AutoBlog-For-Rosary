## Construction and Materials

Each pendant is struck from alloy with a polished finish, and the engraving is applied to both faces. At 2.2cm, detail work is where cheap pieces fall apart — shallow stamping turns into a blur once the piece is in a customer's hand. Clear, legible relief on both sides is what keeps a pendant sellable at a gift-shop price point rather than a closeout bin.

The lightweight build is deliberate. Daily-wear religious jewelry sits against skin for hours, sometimes under clothing, and weight is one of the first complaints that reaches a retailer's counter. A piece this size stays comfortable on a chain or cord without dragging the neckline.

Alloy is a practical choice for this category: it takes a consistent polish, holds fine relief reasonably well, and keeps unit cost low enough that bulk orders stay viable. What alloy covers, though, is a broad family of base metals and surface treatments. If your market cares about nickel sensitivity, tarnish resistance, or whether the finish is plated versus burnished, those specifics vary by production run — contact us to confirm the exact composition and finish before you commit to a purchase order.

One more thing worth flagging for importers: the polished surface is the part most likely to show handling marks in transit. Bulk packaging choice, which we'll come to next, affects that more than the metal itself.

## Pre-Order Verification

Before you sign off on a bulk order, a short verification list saves a lot of grief. Ask for a physical sample first — images at 2.2cm can flatter relief depth, and you want to see how the engraving reads under normal light, not a studio setup.

Specify what you need confirmed in writing. Base alloy and surface finish, the loop or bail opening so it fits your existing chains, piece weight, and consistency of the engraving alignment across a sample batch. Mixed-box consolidation, individual poly bagging, backing cards, and retail-ready packaging should all be listed on the quote rather than assumed.

Then there's pricing. Bulk pricing tiers are structured by quantity, so the quote you get for 50 pieces won't be the quote for 5,000 — ask for the tier breaks up front and you can plan your retail margin before the invoice arrives. Lead times and shipping terms aren't fixed across every order either; contact us to confirm current availability and scheduling for your destination.

If you're sourcing for a church event or a seasonal program, place the sample request early. Approving a physical piece takes time, and packaging artwork adds another round.
