## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Specifications for this category vary by item, so the first ask is a current spec sheet: pouch dimensions, weight, color assortment ratios, closure finish, and how the goods are packed for shipment. Get those numbers in writing before you build a planogram around them.

Then request a physical sample. Photos on a supplier page tell you what the motif looks like; a sample in your hand tells you whether the kiss-lock tension, the color saturation, and the material weight meet the standard your customers expect. It's a cheap step that prevents an expensive return.

If you're sourcing for a retail chain rather than a single parish, work through these before you commit:

- What is the color mix per carton, and can it be adjusted to your assortment?
- How consistent is the Virgin Mary motif and dye lot across repeat orders?
- Which logo printing method applies to your artwork, and what file format do you need to supply?
- Is the pouch shipped loose, or can it be prepared for retail display?
- What does the quotation look like at your target quantity, and are there bulk pricing tiers above it?
- What are the payment terms and the export documentation for your destination market?

Pricing isn't published as a fixed list — quotations are built around your quantity, chosen colors, and customization requirements. So the honest answer to "what does it cost" is: send your numbers and ask.
