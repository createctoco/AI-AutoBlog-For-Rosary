## Checklist Before You Commit

Before you commit to a container or even a first bulk run, get answers in writing on the points that cause the most post-order friction.

- Yarn composition and the colorfastness standard applied. Ask how the supplier tests it, not just whether it's colorfast.
- Stitch density and reinforcement at the loop and closure. These are the two failure points on a pendant pouch.
- Actual dimensions and unit weight, confirmed per variant. Both affect packaging, carton cube, and freight math.
- Logo embroidery capability: placement, maximum stitch area, and the artwork format we need from you.
- Packaging options — polybag, hangtag, header card, gift box — and how each one shifts your per-unit cost.
- A pre-production sample for OEM orders, plus a stock sample for reference. Photograph both and keep them as your quality baseline.
- MOQ confirmation per colorway. Mixing colors inside a 1,200-piece OEM run may or may not be allowed.

Wondering whether this item fits your channel at all? Take a stock sample at the 12-piece minimum, hand it to a few retail buyers, and let their reaction tell you. That's cheaper than guessing.
