## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Sample first — always. Order a set, wear it, and put it under a lamp for a week. You're checking bead uniformity, how the gold-tone heart and pearl accents are actually set, and whether the plating tone matches what you saw in photos.

Before a bulk commitment, get these confirmed in writing:

- **Chain specification** — length, link style, and clasp type, since these vary by item and affect both fit and perceived quality.
- **Earring findings** — post, hook, or clip; buyers with pierced and unpierced customers often need both.
- **Plating and finish details** — how the gold tone is applied and how it's expected to hold up in wear.
- **Bead and pearl consistency** — whether color and size are matched batch to batch, and how much variation is normal.
- **Packaging format** — poly bag, card, pouch, or gift box, and whether it's included or added at cost.
- **MOQ tiers and bulk pricing** — ask how price breaks are structured above the 12-piece in-stock minimum and whether mixed-color orders qualify.
- **Production and shipping lead times** — especially for OEM runs around Easter or confirmation season.
- **Customization sampling** — whether a pre-production sample is available and what it costs.

None of these are unreasonable asks, and a supplier who answers them clearly is telling you something useful about how the rest of the order will go.
