## Checklist Before You Commit

Sourcing professionals know the sample stage is where orders get saved. Here's what to pin down before you place a bulk order.

**Confirm the specs that aren't published.** Chain length, pendant dimensions, weight and clasp type aren't listed in the source material. Ask for them in writing. If you're listing online, you need these for the product page anyway.

**Request a physical sample.** Photos can't tell you how the relief catches light or how the chain sits. A sample also lets you test tarnish resistance in your own climate — relevant if you're distributing to humid regions.

**Verify the finish through wear simulation.** Ask how the polished surface holds up against sweat, sunscreen and everyday abrasion. Varies by item, so get the supplier's answer specific to this piece.

**Lock your customization brief early.** Engraving depth, placement, packaging dimensions and artwork file formats should all be agreed before production. Changes mid-run are expensive.

**Ask about lead times for both paths.** In-stock and OEM timelines differ significantly. Neither is published here, so contact us to confirm what applies to your quantity and customization level.

**Check export and shipping terms.** Mecrt exports to Europe, North America, South America and beyond, with 24/7 online customer service. Still, get Incoterms, packing method and carton specs in writing — especially if you're consolidating with other SKUs.

One more thing: if you're sourcing for a church procurement role rather than a retail channel, your spec priorities shift. You'll care more about packaging suitability for gifting and less about retail display. Say so upfront — it changes the quote.
