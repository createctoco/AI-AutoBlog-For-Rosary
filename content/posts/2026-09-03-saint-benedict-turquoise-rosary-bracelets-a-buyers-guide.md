## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

If you're ready to move forward, the first step is to stop guessing and start asking. There are a few specifics worth clarifying before you request a quote.

First, confirm the exact bead material. The product is described as turquoise beaded, but whether that refers to dyed stone, resin, or another substrate matters for your pricing and for how the product is described on your own listing. Mecrt's team can provide that specification directly. Lead times, shipping terms, and per-unit cost by order size are the same kind of detail — they all depend on your volume and destination, so they need to be quoted rather than assumed.

Second, ask about the adjustable mechanism. How much wrist-size range does it really cover? Pulling on a sample will tell you more than a photo ever could, so request a pre-production sample, especially if you're planning a custom run. You should also verify how the medal or crucifix is attached to the bracelet. This is a handmade product, and attachment quality affects durability in the hands of a customer who wears it every day.

Finally, talk through color consistency. Natural variations can be charming in boutique retail but problematic for a brand that plans to reorder the same product six months from now. Ask how Mecrt manages tone consistency across production batches and whether bead counts or medal finishes can be locked into your order documentation. For custom orders, wrap all of those decisions into a written specification before you approve a sample.
