## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk order, pin down the physical specifications. The source material doesn't publish bead diameter, cross dimensions, bracelet circumference, clasp type, or unit weight, and those numbers drive everything from your packaging choice to your shipping cost. Ask for a spec sheet in writing and hold the supplier to it on the purchase order.

Sample first. Request a production sample — not just a photo — so you can assess the hematite finish, the weight in hand, and the quality of the cross centerpiece. Check how the holy soil element is incorporated and described, since that detail carries both spiritual and marketing weight and you'll need to represent it accurately to your own customers.

Then work through the commercial terms: current bulk pricing tiers, the exact MOQ for your configuration, lead time on both stock and custom orders, packaging options and their cost impact, and shipping terms to your destination. None of these are published figures, so contact Mecrt directly to confirm them for your order size.

Ask about consistency, too. If you're reordering seasonally, request confirmation that bead color and finish will match across batches — natural stone varies, and a shade shift between shipments can hurt you at retail.
