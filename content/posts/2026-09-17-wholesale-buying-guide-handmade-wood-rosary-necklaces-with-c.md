## Checklist Before You Commit

Run through this before you sign off on a bulk order. It's short, and each item prevents a specific kind of problem.

**Confirm the physical specs.** Bead size, cord type, total length, pendant dimensions, and metal finish — request these in writing and match them to your retail listing. Vague specs cause returns, and returns on religious goods are awkward for everyone.

**Order a sample first.** With a 12-piece in-stock minimum, sampling is cheap. Wear it, flex the cord, check the pendant attachment, and see how the wood grain varies across units. Photograph it next to a ruler for your own product listings.

**Verify packaging options.** If you need retail-ready boxes or branded inserts, confirm what's included at the standard tier versus what requires the 1,200-piece custom run. Packaging isn't always quoted in the base price.

**Ask about pricing tiers and payment terms.** Get the tier structure in writing, along with whatever terms the supplier offers. Don't assume — ask.

**Check consistency across batches.** For repeat orders, ask how the supplier handles color and grain variation between production runs. If your customers expect uniformity, that's a conversation to have now.

**Confirm export and shipping details.** Mecrt exports to Europe, North America, and South America, so international shipping is routine. Still, confirm Incoterms, documentation, and who handles customs for your destination.

Wondering whether your market can support the 1,200-piece custom tier? Start with the 12-piece in-stock order, test sell-through, then scale. That sequence costs little and tells you more than any sales deck.
