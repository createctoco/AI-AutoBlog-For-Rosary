**Pendant weight and dimensions.** These aren't published in the current product material. For men's jewelry especially, weight drives perceived value, and your listing copy will need accurate figures. Request measured samples rather than relying on a description.

**Finish consistency.** If you're ordering both polished and brushed versions, ask how the two are separated in packing and whether finish is specified on the carton labeling. Mixed-finish shipments without clear labeling create fulfillment headaches.

**Sample policy.** Always order a sample before a 1,200-piece run. Confirm whether sample cost is credited against the bulk order and how long the sample takes to reach you.

**Compliance and documentation.** If your market requires material declarations or import documentation for jewelry, ask what Mecrt can provide. Don't assume; confirm before the order is placed.

**Lead time.** Delivery timelines are quoted per order, so get yours in writing along with the payment terms.
