## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk order, get the specification questions answered in writing. A few that matter most:

**Finish and plating.** Polished and antique-toned are the two listed options, but confirm the exact surface treatment, how it's applied, and whether it holds up to handling and light abrasion. Request physical swatches, not photos.

**Dimensions and weight.** These aren't published here, so contact Mecrt to confirm the pendant size, thickness, and unit weight against your packaging and shipping calculations.

**Keyring and loop specification.** Ask about the keyring type, the loop opening, and the attachment method. If you're assembling into rosaries or necklaces, the loop diameter determines what chain or cord you can use.

**Packaging and carton data.** Poly bag, backing card, display box — confirm what's standard and what's custom. Get units per carton and carton dimensions for freight planning.

**Pricing tiers and lead times.** In-stock MOQ is 12 pieces and custom OEM MOQ is 1,200 pieces, but pricing scales with volume and lead times vary by order. Submit your target quantity and required date with your inquiry so the wholesale team can respond with something concrete.
