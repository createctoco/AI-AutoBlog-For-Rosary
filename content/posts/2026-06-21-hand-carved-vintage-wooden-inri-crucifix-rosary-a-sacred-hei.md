### Knots Matter More Than You’d Think

Here’s something a lot of buyers overlook: the knots.  

We can do either Peruvian knot or flat knot, depending on what you prefer. Peruvian knot is more traditional — gives the rosary a flexible, almost chain-like feel. Flat knot is tighter, more rigid. Some customers tell us flat knot lasts longer. Others say Peruvian knot feels better in hand.  

Honestly? Both are fine. We’ve been making both for years, and we haven’t seen a clear winner in terms of durability. It really comes down to personal preference.  

If you’re ordering for a specific community, ask them what they’re used to. We’ll match it.

### A Note on Quality Control

We hand-check every single rosary before packing. Not kidding.  

Each bead gets a visual inspection. Each knot gets pulled to make sure it’s tight. The crucifix gets checked for cracks or loose fittings. This slows down our packing line, but we’d rather ship 100 good rosaries than 1,000 bad ones.  

If you’ve ever ordered religious goods from overseas, you know the risk. Some factories batch-produce rosaries so fast the beads chip within a month. Ours don’t.
