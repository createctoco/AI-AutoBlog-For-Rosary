## Design and Craft Details

The design is intentionally minimal — a round pine bead with a centered hole for stringing. But minimal doesn't mean inconsistent. Mecrt emphasizes consistent sizing and finish on bulk orders, which is the difference between a professional product line and a hobby bin.

The surface is smooth and polished, with the wood's natural grain left visible. That's an aesthetic choice as much as a practical one. Buyers who market to eco-conscious customers want to see organic texture, not plastic-looking uniformity. Each bead carries slight variation in grain, which gives handmade pieces their character while staying within an acceptable range for commercial consistency.

Two processing details matter for wholesale buyers:

- **Staining.** Natural pine is light, which means you have a clean canvas. Staining lets you match a specific brand palette or create a uniform color across a prayer bead set.
- **Polishing.** A polished surface resists dirt and oil from handling, and it makes stringing easier — rough beads snag cord and slow down assembly.

Both options are available as customization services, and you can also request custom packaging to match your retail presentation.

## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Before you send an inquiry, get specific. Here's what a sourcing professional should confirm:

- **Bead diameter and hole size.** The product page doesn't list standard dimensions. If your design uses a particular cord gauge, you'll need to verify the hole diameter can accommodate it. A one-millimeter difference can change your entire stringing process.
- **Quantity per wholesale unit.** How many beads come in a unit? This affects your per-piece cost calculation and your packaging plan.
- **Finish consistency across lots.** Wood is a natural material. If you're placing repeat orders, ask how much color and grain variation to expect between batches.
- **MOQ terms.** Stock starts at 12 pieces; custom starts at 1,200. Confirm which applies to your project and whether samples count toward a larger order.
- **Customization scope.** Staining, polishing, and packaging are mentioned as options. Clarify what's included in the quote and what's an add-on.
- **Lead time and shipping.** The source doesn't state production or transit times. For a supplier exporting to Europe, North America, and South America, confirm delivery dates before you commit.

Pricing isn't listed on the page — it's quote-based. That's normal for wholesale components, but it means your inquiry should include target volume, intended use, and any customization needs so Mecrt can return a tailored quotation.
