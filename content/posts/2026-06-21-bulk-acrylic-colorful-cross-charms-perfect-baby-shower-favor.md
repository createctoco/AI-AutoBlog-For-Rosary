## Small Cross Charms for Baby Shower Favors – A Different Kind of Bulk Order

We get a lot of requests that aren’t just rosaries. Sometimes it’s parishes stocking up for baptism season. Other times it’s Catholic moms planning a baby shower who want something with real meaning. Not the generic plastic trinkets you find at party stores. Something with a cross.

Last month a woman from Texas ordered 2,000 colorful small cross charms for her daughter’s baptism party favors. She wanted them bright, cheerful, but still reverent. That’s the balance we try to hit every time.

So here’s the thing about bulk acrylic cross charms for baby shower favors – you can do it cheap, or you can do it right. We’ve been making these for years. Let’s talk about what actually works.

### Why Acrylic? Why Small?

Acrylic is light. For a baby shower favor, you don’t want something heavy that costs a fortune to ship. You also don’t want something that breaks if a toddler drops it. Acrylic holds up.

The small size matters too. We’re talking crosses maybe 1.5 to 2 inches. Fits in a palm. Tucks into a favor bag. Can be attached to a card or tied with ribbon. The 8mm bead size on our rosaries is our biggest seller for a reason – people like things that feel substantial but not bulky. Same logic applies here.

These aren’t heirloom pieces. They’re a little token of faith. A reminder of the baptism. Something guests can keep in their pocket, on their keychain, or tucked in a Bible.

### What We Can Do With Color

We’ve done pastel sets for baby showers – pink, light blue, mint green, soft yellow. Looks great with a white ribbon. We’ve also done bright rainbow mixes for parish events where kids are involved. Honestly, the most popular color for baptism favors is still white or clear with a gold accent. But we can mix it up.

The acrylic material takes color well. No fading. No chipping. And because it’s not wood or metal, you don’t have to worry about allergies or tarnishing.

If you want something a little more upscale, we also make wooden crosses. Our real solid wood stuff – hand-carved, polished, no plastic imitation. But for baby shower favors, acrylic is usually the better call. Lighter, cheaper, more cheerful.

### On Customization and Minimums

Here’s where people get confused.

You want your church name or the baby’s name on the packaging? Or maybe a little card attached? We can do that. We’ve printed parish names, scripture verses, even individual baby names on the hang tags.

The MOQ for custom packaging with your logo is 1200 pieces. That sounds like a lot. But for a church that does multiple baptisms a year? Or a Catholic bookstore stocking up for baptism season? It goes fast.

For our in-stock acrylic crosses, you can start at just 12 pieces. Need 50 for a baby shower? Done. Need 500 for a parish event? Also done. Buy more, pay less – that’s how it works.

### The Knot Question – Yes, We Can Do That

Some people want the cross on a cord. Some want it on a keychain. Some just want the cross loose in a bag.

If you want it on a cord, we can tie it with a Peruvian knot or a flat knot. Peruvian knot is cleaner, doesn’t slip. Flat knot is more traditional, looks like the knots on a rosary. We match it to the design.

For baby shower favors, we usually recommend a simple satin ribbon or a thin leather cord. Easy to wear. Doesn’t look like a necklace – more like a bracelet or a charm.

### What About Baptism vs. Baby Shower?

We make these for both. The difference is usually in the packaging.

For a baptism, people want something a little more formal. Maybe a small box. Maybe a card with the baptism date and the child’s name.

For a baby shower, it’s more casual. A little organza bag. A tag that says “Thank you for celebrating with us.” Sometimes people just put the crosses on a table with a sign.

We’ve also done funeral favors with these crosses. White or black. Simple. Respectful. People keep them in memory of the deceased. It’s a small comfort.

### One Thing Nobody Tells You About Bulk Crosses

The cheap ones from China? They break. The paint peels. The cross looks more like an alien symbol than a crucifix.

We’ve been doing this long enough to know what holds up. Our acrylic crosses are solid. No hollow backs. No cheap plating. The color goes all the way through.

Is it the fanciest thing you’ve ever seen? No. But it’s honest. And for a baby shower favor, that’s what matters.
