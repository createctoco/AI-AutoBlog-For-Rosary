## Design and Craft Details

Every bead is solid pine, selected for light weight and a smooth grain. At 8mm they read as a comfortable in-between size — substantial in the hand without feeling bulky on the wrist. The finish is natural wood rather than a heavy lacquer, so tone varies slightly from bead to bead. Some buyers call that rustic character; others treat it as a spec they need to control. Either way, it's a detail worth settling with your account contact before committing to a large run, including whether the wood is left raw or lightly treated.

The rope is the other half of the story. It's woven by hand, which gives the bracelet a texture you don't get from a molded cord and adds real durability at the flex point. Hand weaving also lends the piece a tactile quality that matches the organic look of the pine. The cross is attached securely, and the whole bracelet is assembled to hold consistent sizing and finish across wholesale orders — the point that matters most when you're receiving five thousand units rather than five.

For church procurement, there's a practical benefit hidden in that simplicity. No clasp, no plating to tarnish, no battery, nothing to service. It stores flat and doesn't demand special handling.

## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Come to the conversation with a short list. These are the ones that tend to matter most on a wooden devotional bracelet:

- **Bead finish.** Raw, oiled, or waxed? Ask for the exact treatment and a photo of a current production batch.
- **Bead consistency.** What tolerance is held on the 8mm diameter, and how is color variation managed within a single run?
- **Rope details.** Hand-woven, confirmed — but ask about cord color options and how the weave is finished at the ends.
- **Cross attachment.** How is the pendant secured? Request a close-up, and a pull test if these are headed to children's programs.
- **Sizing.** Confirm the bangle's inner circumference and whether it stretches or runs fixed. Varies by item — contact us to confirm.
- **Packaging.** Poly bag, organza pouch, gift box, display card? Custom packaging is available at the custom MOQ.
- **MOQ and pricing tiers.** 12 pieces for stock, 1,200 for custom. Unit pricing depends on quantity, configuration and packaging — request a quote for your target volume.
- **Lead time and shipping.** Not published as a fixed figure; confirm against your in-hands date.
- **Compliance paperwork.** If you need documentation for your market, raise it early so it can be prepared alongside the order.

A pre-production sample is the cheapest insurance you'll buy. Ask for one that reflects the exact finish, cord color and packaging you've specified — not a shelf sample pulled from stock.
