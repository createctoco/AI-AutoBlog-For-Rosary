### The Size That Wins

We make these medals in 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm diameters. The 10mm is the sweet spot — small enough to fit on a bracelet or keychain, big enough to actually read the saint’s name. The 8mm works for very delicate chain bracelets, but honest opinion? People complain it’s too tiny to see clearly. The 12mm starts looking chunky unless you’re doing a chunky piece.

Each medal is stamped from solid brass or zinc alloy, then hand-finished. No cheap plastic coating that peels after a month. We’ve been doing this long enough to know what holds up when someone wears it daily.

Our standard finish is antique silver or antique bronze. You want gold tone? We can do that. You want a matte finish? Also doable. But the antique finishes outsell everything else 3-to-1. Something about the vintage look makes people feel like it’s been blessed longer.
