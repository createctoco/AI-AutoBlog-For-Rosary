### What Makes Antiqued Pewter Work for St. Francis Medals

The antiqued finish gives it a vintage look. It doesn’t scream “brand new factory item.” It looks like it could’ve been pulled out of a drawer in an old rectory. That’s exactly what many Catholic buyers want — something that feels grounded, traditional, maybe a little worn.

Pewter itself is durable. It doesn’t tarnish like silver or turn your skin green like cheap alloys. And the weight is nice — not too heavy, but you know it’s there. For a medal like St. Francis, which is often given as a gift for pet blessings, confirmation, or even funeral memorials, that durability matters.
