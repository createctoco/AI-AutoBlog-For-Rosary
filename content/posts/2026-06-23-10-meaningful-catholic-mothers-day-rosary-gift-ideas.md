### Why Wooden Rosaries Work for Mother’s Day

Mothers appreciate the tactile feel of real wood. Not plastic. Not resin pretending to be wood. Our beads are cut from solid timber — natural grain, warm to the touch, and they age beautifully. Some moms keep a rosary in their purse for decades.

We hand-carve and polish each bead. No machine stamping here. That means every rosary has slight variations — a feature, not a flaw. It’s proof a human made it.

Size matters too. Our 8mm beads are the most popular for daily use. 10mm feels more substantial — good for older hands or arthritis. 6mm? Too small for most adults. 12mm? That’s a statement piece, but not for everyday carrying.

For Mother’s Day, we see a lot of orders for the 8mm size. Lightweight, comfortable, easy to pray with.

Parish gift shops love this: we can put your church name or parish logo on the packaging. Or on a small metal tag attached to the rosary. Some buyers want the crucifix engraved. Others just want a simple card inside the box.

### Practical Tips for Church Procurement

Buying for a parish? Here’s what we’ve learned from talking to dozens of church buyers:

- **Order early.** March is ideal. April is cutting it close. May is panic mode.
- **Test a small batch first.** Order 12 pieces of a stock rosary. See how they feel, how they pray. Then commit to a larger OEM run.
- **Think about packaging.** We offer simple poly bags, velvet pouches, or gift boxes. Pouches are popular for Mother’s Day — adds a premium feel without much cost.
- **Consider the crucifix.** Some moms prefer a traditional crucifix. Others like a more modern design. We’ve got both.

One thing we hear a lot: “The rosary needs to feel good in the hand.” That’s why we don’t skimp on bead finishing. Each bead is sanded smooth, then polished with natural wax. No rough edges. No splinters.
