## Checklist Before You Commit

Wondering whether to place a trial order or go straight to a custom run? Work through a short list first.

Request a physical sample. Product photography flatters relief work, and you want to see how the figure reads under normal light, not studio lighting. Check the polish, the edge finish, and whether the bail or loop feels solid.

Confirm the specification sheet in writing. That includes dimensions, weight, material confirmation, and whether a chain or cord is included. If it's not stated in the source material, don't assume — ask.

Verify engraving legibility if you're ordering customized pieces. Small text on a curved surface is the most common place custom runs go wrong, and fixing it after production is expensive.

Ask about packaging before you finalize pricing. Poly bag, velvet pouch, and gift box each change both your unit cost and your retail positioning.

Get your MOQ confirmed per variant. If you want the pendant in multiple finishes or with several packaging options, minimums may apply per configuration rather than per order.

Request your quotation with a validity window attached. Metal and material costs move, and a quote from three months ago may not hold.
