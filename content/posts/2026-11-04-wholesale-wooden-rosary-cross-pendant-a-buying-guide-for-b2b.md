Before you commit to a bulk run, verify these points with the supplier directly:

- **Spec sheet confirmation.** Ask for bead diameter tolerance and pendant dimensions in writing, since "8mm" and "handmade" coexist with normal handmade variance.
- **Strand assembly.** Confirm how the beads are strung and how the piece fastens — those details vary by item, so get them documented.
- **Sampling.** Request a sample before a large custom run. Check weight, grain appearance, and how the pendant sits when worn.
- **Color and size options.** The wooden range includes multiple sizes and finishes; ask which apply to this specific SKU.
- **Packaging and labeling.** Confirm whether individual poly bags, printed cards, or gift boxes are included or quoted separately.
- **Lead time.** This varies by order volume and customization depth, so pin it down at the quote stage rather than after deposit.
- **Logo engraving feasibility.** Send your artwork early and ask what file format and size work best on a wooden pendant.

Anyone sourcing Catholic goods knows the failure mode: a beautiful sample, then a bulk shipment that drifts on size or finish. Get the specs in writing and inspect a production sample before the full run.
