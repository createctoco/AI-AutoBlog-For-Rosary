## What to Confirm with the Supplier

The listing title and the product description don't point to the same material — the title reads "plastic prayer cross," while the body copy describes handmade natural wood. Resolve that before you quote anything to your own customers. Ask for the current material spec in writing and a photo of an actual production unit, not the studio shot.

Beyond material, several specs deserve a direct question:

- Finish and grain consistency across batches, especially if you reorder seasonally
- Key ring hardware — metal type, plating, and how it holds up to daily pocket wear
- Unit weight and packed dimensions, for freight and FBA planning
- Packaging formats available: poly bag, backing card, gift box, custom printed
- Sample lead time versus bulk lead time — neither is published, so ask
- Export documentation and labeling requirements for your destination market
- Whether custom packaging carries its own MOQ separate from the 1,200-piece OEM threshold

Order samples first. Handmade goods vary, and a sample is the only reliable way to judge whether the tactile finish meets the standard your customers expect. One piece won't tell you much about batch consistency, so ask whether they can send several drawn from different production runs.
