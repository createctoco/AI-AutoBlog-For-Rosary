## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you commit to a bulk order, confirm the plating specification you're buying. Gold plating varies in thickness, and thickness is what determines how the finish behaves over time. Ask Mecrt directly rather than assuming.

Pendant dimensions and weight, chain length, and clasp type should all be confirmed in writing, since these are the details that generate returns. If you're specifying altered pendant size or chain length, request a sample first and compare it against the standard version.

Ask how sampling works — whether a sample is charged, and whether that cost is credited against your production order. Then ask how the supplier keeps production consistent with the approved sample, and what the process is if a shipment doesn't match. Quality control terms are worth understanding before the first order, not after.

Other items on your checklist: packaging format and whether custom packaging is quoted separately, labeling requirements for your market, and the export documentation Mecrt provides. Pricing at different volumes is worth discussing as one conversation — ask how pricing scales as quantities move from the stock MOQ of 12 pieces toward the 1,200-piece custom threshold.
