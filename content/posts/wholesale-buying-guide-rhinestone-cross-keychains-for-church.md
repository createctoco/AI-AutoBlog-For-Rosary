## Material and Design Highlights

Three elements do the work here: the polished zinc alloy frame, the rhinestone accents, and the drip oil finish. Zinc alloy gives the piece a solid feel in the hand — the kind of weight shoppers associate with something that isn't disposable. Rhinestones add sparkle at the cross pendant, and the drip oil coating produces a smooth, glossy surface over the colored areas.

The key ring is deliberately compact. It attaches easily to keys, bag straps, or small gift sets, and that flexibility is why the item travels well across formats — standalone accessory, part of an assortment pack, or a component inside a holiday gift box.

Design restraint is the selling point. A rhinestone cross can easily tip into something heavy-handed; here the metal frame keeps the silhouette clean while the sparkle stays at accent level. That balance works on a pegboard where a case needs a highlight piece, and it works in e-commerce, where a glossy finish photographs well under basic lighting without a full studio setup.

## What to Verify Before Ordering

Don't guess on specifications. The product page covers the construction and styling, but several details a sourcing professional needs are order-specific — and the fastest route is a direct question rather than an assumption.

**Colorways.** The available range isn't published in full. Request the current list for the period you plan to ship in.

**Samples.** Ask for a physical sample before a bulk commitment. Check plating color, rhinestone quality, drip oil coverage, and the split ring's tension in person.

**Lead times.** These vary by order volume and by whether customization is involved. Get a written estimate against your quantity and target delivery date.

**Packaging.** Ask what's standard — polybag, backing card, gift box — and what's possible on a custom run. Packaging choices affect both your landed cost and how the item presents on a shelf or in a listing photo.

**Unit weight and dimensions.** Useful for freight math and for e-commerce spec fields. Contact us to confirm current figures rather than working from estimates.

**Rhinestone retention.** Ask how the stones are secured, then test it on your sample. This is the most common failure point on this type of accessory.

**Order volume pricing.** Send your quantity bands and the platform you sell on; a quotation built around your volume is more accurate than a single unit figure.
