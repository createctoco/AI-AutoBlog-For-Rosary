## Checklist Before You Commit

A few things to nail down before you wire a deposit. None of them are unusual, but skipping them is how buyers end up with 20,000 pendants nobody wants.

**Sample first.** Ask for a sample of the actual mix, not a photo. You're evaluating relief depth, edge finish, and whether the oval profile holds consistent across the batch. Sight and feel still decide these things.

**Get the saint list in writing.** "Mix" means different things to different suppliers. Confirm which saints are included, in what proportion, and whether you can weight the mix toward the figures your market actually requests.

**Confirm the finish.** Bright plating and antique plating photograph very differently and suit different price points. Ask which is standard for the in-stock mix, and how a change affects price and timing.

**Check the dimensions.** Since sizing isn't published, request exact oval dimensions and unit weight. If these charms have to fit an existing bezel, jump ring or cord diameter, that's a hard constraint — better settled by email than by a returned shipment.

**Ask about plating durability.** For wearable devotional jewelry, plating wear is the most common end-user complaint. Ask how the finish performs in normal use and what your options are if you need something more resistant.

**Clarify packaging and labeling.** Custom packaging is available. Whether that means poly bags, header cards or retail boxes carrying your brand — ask, and get the per-unit cost implication.

**Request lead time in writing.** It isn't listed in the source material and will depend on quantity plus customization level, so have it stated alongside your quotation.
