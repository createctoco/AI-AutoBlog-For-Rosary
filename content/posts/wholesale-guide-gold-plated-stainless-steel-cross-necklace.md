## Pre-Order Verification

Order a sample. That’s the short version. The longer version is knowing what to look at when it lands.

Start with the engraving. Hold the pendant at reading distance and check that the Lord’s Prayer is fully legible, evenly spaced, and clean at the letter edges. Then check the plating: consistent tone across the face and the edges, no bubbling at the bail, no patchiness where the cross arms meet the center. Ordering several colors? Compare them side by side — finish drift between colorways is a common problem in plated runs.

Work the clasp a dozen times. Confirm the chain gauge feels right for the pendant weight and that the pendant hangs straight rather than twisting. If your channel needs retail-ready packaging, ask for a packaging sample as well, not just a photo. Wondering whether a sample order is worth the freight? At a 12-piece minimum it usually is.
