## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Before you commit to a bulk run, get these answered in writing. It's the difference between a smooth program and a shipment you can't sell.

**Confirm the exact configuration.** Wood species and bead color aren't specified in the standard listing, so ask. If your market skews toward a particular wood tone, verify before you order 5,000 pieces.

**Ask about engraving tolerances.** Custom engraving on a cross or centerpiece has practical limits — text length, line count, and detail resolution all matter. Send your artwork or text and get confirmation that it reproduces cleanly at production scale. This is where custom orders most often go sideways.

**Request a sample before the bulk run.** For a stock order, a sample confirms the hand feel, bead finish, and crucifix attachment quality. For a custom order, it's non-negotiable — you need to see your engraving and packaging before committing. Sample cost and turnaround vary by item, so confirm with the supplier.

**Verify packaging specs.** If you're supplying a retail channel, packaging dimensions and presentation affect how the product displays and ships. Ask what the default packaging is and what changes under a custom program.

**Clarify lead times and shipping for your destination.** These vary by order size, customization level, and destination market. Get them in writing rather than working from assumptions.

**Check the minimum order quantities against your plan.** In-stock MOQ is 12 pieces; OEM and custom MOQ is 1,200 pieces.
