## The Logistics of Bulk Church Orders (What Nobody Tells You)

We’ve been doing this long enough to see the same mistakes repeat.

**Mistake #1: Underestimating lead time.** Our OEM custom orders — where you want your parish name or church logo on the packaging or the hang tag — require a 1200-piece minimum. That’s not arbitrary. We set up the production line, we source the materials, we coordinate the hand-carving schedule. It takes 45-60 days depending on the season.

If you need 1,200 rosaries with “St. Mary’s Cathedral” printed on the card by December 1st? You should have called us in September. Seriously.

**Mistake #2: Ordering too many of one size.** We see this all the time. Someone orders 2,000 pieces of 12mm rosaries because they think bigger is better. Then they realize 12mm beads are bulky — hard to hold for elderly hands, awkward for kids. Our best seller is 8mm. Then 10mm. Then 6mm for small gift sets. 12mm has its place (men’s pocket rosaries, outdoor use) but don’t bet the whole order on it.

**Mistake #3: Forgetting about knot preference.** This sounds minor until you’re holding 500 rosaries where the knots don’t match your parish’s tradition. We can do Peruvian knot — that’s the tight, durable style with visible cross-pattern. Or flat knot — smoother, sits flatter in the hand. Some customers even ask for a mix. We just need to know upfront.

## How We Actually Make These Things

Walk through our workshop and you won’t see injection molding machines. You won’t hear the hiss of plastic cooling. What you’ll see is wood — real wood, sourced from sustainable suppliers — being cut, drilled, shaped, sanded, polished.

Each bead goes through human hands. Not a robot arm. Not a CNC press. A person. We’ve got craftsmen who’ve been doing this for twenty years. They know how hard to press the sandpaper, how long to polish each facet, how tight to pull the cord so it doesn’t slip.

That’s why our rosaries don’t look like the factory-made ones from Shenzhen. They’ve got character. Slight variations in wood grain. Different light catches. That’s not a defect — that’s evidence of handwork.

For Christmas orders, we also do special packaging. Red velvet pouches. Gold-embossed cards. Custom inserts with the Christmas gospel verse. If you’re buying 1,200 pieces, we can make the packaging match your church’s aesthetic.

## A Quick Word on Quality Control

We test every batch. Not random sampling — every single rosary gets checked. Knot tension. Bead finish. Crucifix alignment. If a bead cracks during stringing, it gets replaced. If a knot loosens, it gets retied.

This matters for Christmas orders because you’re not the end user. The end user is someone’s grandmother, someone’s godchild, someone who just received a gift from their parish. If that rosary breaks on Christmas morning, it reflects on you. Not on us.
