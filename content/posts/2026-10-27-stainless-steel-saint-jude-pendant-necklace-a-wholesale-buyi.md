## Design and Craft Details

The base metal is stainless steel with gold plating applied over it. Stainless is the practical choice for a piece meant to be worn daily — it holds its shape, keeps a comfortable weight on the neck, and resists tarnish with normal care. The plating provides the finish; the steel core provides the durability. Neither part works without the other.

Where the piece earns its margin is the figure itself. Saint Jude is rendered in gold plated detail against the polished steel ground, and the contrast between figure and background is what a customer actually sees at arm's length. Ask for close-up photos from the production run, not just a sample — plating definition is usually the first thing to soften when a workshop is pushing volume.

A word on care, since your retail customers will ask. "Resists tarnish with normal care" means keeping the piece away from perfume, chlorine and prolonged sweat exposure, and drying it after wear. ## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Before you commit to a bulk run, pin down the specifications in writing. These are the items that cause most of the disputes we hear about in this product category:

- **Exact dimensions.** Chain length and pendant size in millimetres. Get it on the quotation, not just in a chat thread.
- **Chain style and clasp type.** A lobster clasp and a spring ring read very differently to your customer, and they cost differently too.
- **Plating specification.** Ask what finish is being applied and whether wear-testing data is available. If it isn't, say so in your own product copy.
- **Packaging options.** Poly bag, pouch, gift box or custom printed card. Packaging is often where the per-unit cost actually moves, so price it separately.
- **A production sample.** Request one from an actual production run rather than a showroom piece. It's the only way to judge weight, plating tone and figure definition honestly.
- **Batch consistency.** If you plan to reorder, ask how plating colour is controlled between runs. A slightly different gold on the second order creates customer service problems you didn't budget for.
- **Lead time and shipping terms.** These vary by quantity, finish and destination — contact us to confirm against your delivery window rather than assuming a standard.

If you're sourcing for a church procurement office rather than a retail chain, ask about how smaller follow-on orders are handled. The 12-piece in-stock MOQ is useful for replenishing a bookshop display without holding stock you won't move until the next feast day.
