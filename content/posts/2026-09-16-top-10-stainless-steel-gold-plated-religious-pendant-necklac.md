## Checklist Before You Commit

Run through these before you sign off on a bulk order:

- **Confirm the material spec in writing.** Base metal, plating, and finish should be documented — not lifted from a product photo.
- **Ask for a pre-production sample.** Photography flatters plating. Handle the piece before committing to 1,200 units.
- **Pin down the physical details.** Chain length, pendant size, and clasp style vary by item; have them stated on your quotation.
- **Clarify which MOQ tier applies.** A 12-piece stock reorder and a 1,200-piece custom run move on different timelines and different pricing.
- **Spell out customization limits upfront.** Engraving area, artwork formats, and packaging specs need agreement before production starts.
- **Ask for lead times and volume breakpoints.** These are quoted per order, so ask for the breaks that actually apply to your quantities.
- **Check your market's labeling requirements.** If you're importing into Europe, North America, or South America, confirm what documentation your channel needs.
