### Why Real Wood Matters for a Chaplet

Our Small Chaplet of Divine Mercy rosaries use **natural solid wood** — not the resin or plastic imitation stuff you see on budget imports.  

Why does that matter? Wood feels warmer in the hand. It doesn’t get slick when your fingers are moving through the prayers. And for older parishioners or those with arthritis, the slight texture of hand-carved beads makes a real difference.  

Each bead is **hand-polished**, not machine-stamped. That means no two beads are exactly identical. Some people notice that right away. Others just know it feels “right” when they pray.  

We offer these chaplets in **8mm and 10mm** sizes. The 8mm is our best seller — comfortable for most hands, fits easily in a pocket or purse. The 10mm has a bit more weight, which some people prefer for longer prayer sessions.  

We can also do **6mm** (small, delicate) or **12mm** (large, substantial), but those are less common for chaplets.
