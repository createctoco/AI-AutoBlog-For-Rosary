## Construction and Materials

The heart centrepiece is zircon, set to read as a vivid red against the muted metal around it. That contrast is the whole point of the design: the plating is warm and antique, the stone is saturated, so the earrings hold their look under natural daylight and under the harder light of a display case. Anyone who's worked with plated fashion jewelry knows how easily a dark finish turns muddy — the color break here is what keeps the piece legible from a distance.

Base metal is zinc alloy, copper-plated. Zinc alloy takes fine detail well, which matters on the flame and thorn accents where the lines run thin and close together. Weight has been kept down deliberately so the earrings can be worn through a full day rather than saved for an evening. For a pendant drop, that's a practical consideration — heavy pairs sell once and come back as complaints.

Each unit is hand-finished before packing. On a wholesale run, that's where you'll see small variables between pieces: slight tonal shifts in the plated surface, minor shade differences in the zircon. Both are normal in plated costume jewelry and won't change the overall look, but they're worth knowing if your channel photographs product at high magnification.

Durability is the honest question with any copper-plated item. The metalwork is built to hold its intricate detail through normal wear, and the plating is the wear point over longer periods — as with any plated alloy, contact with perfume, sweat and humidity will shorten its life. That's a care-label matter rather than a defect, and worth flagging on your packaging or product listing.

## Pre-Order Verification

Before you place a bulk order, pin down a few things.

Ask for a sample or a pre-production unit. Photos flatten plated surfaces and exaggerate stone color, and the only reliable way to judge the antique finish and the true red of the zircon is in your hand.

Get the backing type and drop dimensions confirmed in writing. The published design notes describe the piece but not exact measurements, so contact us to confirm — don't work from assumed numbers when you're planning packaging or building a listing template.

Check how the zircon is set. Prong, bezel and adhesive settings behave differently over time and at different price points, and the answer affects how the item handles returns.

Sort out packaging and mixed sizing early if you need either, and ask for the MOQ on your specific configuration in writing — 12 pieces applies to in-stock, 1,200 to OEM, but a mixed-size or custom-packaged order can shift that.

Finally, if you're ordering several SKUs for a seasonal buy, ask what can be consolidated into one shipment. Combining a gothic or gift assortment usually keeps freight sensible.
