### What Goes Into Our Braided Cord Rosaries

Here’s something you won’t hear from every supplier:

**Our beads are real solid wood.** Not plastic. Not resin pretending to be wood. We use natural timber — olive wood, rosewood, sometimes mahogany depending on the order. Each bead gets hand-carved and polished by our craftsmen. That’s not marketing talk. That’s how we’ve always done it.

The cord itself? Braided nylon or polyester — strong enough to hold up to daily use. We can finish it with either a **Peruvian knot** or a **flat knot**, whichever your customers prefer. The Peruvian knot looks more traditional. The flat knot sits flatter against clothing. Your call.

**Size matters too.** For single decade rosaries, 8mm beads are our sweet spot. 10mm works if you want something more substantial. 6mm? A bit small for older hands. 12mm? That’s pushing it for a pocket rosary — starts feeling bulky.
