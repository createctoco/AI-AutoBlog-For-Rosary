## Company Background

**Mecrt (Yiwu) International Trade Co., Ltd** is a Catholic rosary manufacturer and wholesale supplier headquartered in Yiwu, Zhejiang Province, China. Founded in 2015, the company has grown from a small workshop into a production operation with over 160 skilled artisans and a monthly output exceeding 100,000 units. Its product catalog spans ten major categories — Catholic rosaries, rosary bracelets, scapulars, religious accessories, ornaments, fashion necklaces, keychains, rings, brooches, and jewelry packaging — with thousands of SKU variations.

Yiwu is known globally as the largest small commodities trading hub on the planet. For a rosary manufacturer, this location provides direct access to raw material suppliers, packaging vendors, and logistics networks within a short radius. That proximity keeps material costs down and lead times short, which is part of the reason Mecrt has been able to maintain wholesale prices between $0.15 and $2.00 per unit across its product range.

The company serves clients in more than 30 countries, including the United States, Mexico, Brazil, the Philippines, Italy, and Poland. Its customer base includes Amazon private-label sellers, church supply distributors, religious gift shops, pilgrimage souvenir retailers, and wholesale importers.

### How the Workshop Operates

Visitors to the production floor often notice what is missing: conveyor belts, robotic arms, automated assembly lines. Instead, the workspace consists of tables covered with bead trays, cord spools, crucifixes, and centerpieces — all assembled by hand.

The manufacturing process at **Mecrt (Yiwu) International Trade Co., Ltd** relies on traditional hand-weaving and knotting techniques. Each rosary passes through multiple stages: bead selection and polishing, cord cutting and knotting, crucifix and centerpiece attachment, quality inspection, and packaging. The Peruvian knot rosaries — one of the company's signature product lines — require a specific hand-weaving technique that takes roughly two weeks to learn and several months to master.

Quality control happens at three points: raw material inspection, mid-production checks during knotting and assembly, and a final pre-shipment inspection. The company provides pre-shipment photos and videos to buyers so they can verify product appearance before goods leave the factory.

## Product Lines and Materials

### Wooden Catholic Rosaries

The wooden rosary is Mecrt's core product. These are made with solid natural wood beads — not plastic or resin imitation material. The difference is tactile: real wood has weight, warms in the hand during prayer, and develops a personal patina over time as it absorbs trace amounts of skin oil. The grain pattern varies slightly from bead to bead because each one is cut from actual timber.

Bead sizes run from 6mm up to 12mm, with 8mm and 10mm being the most commonly produced sizes. The 8mm beads suit daily carry and pocket rosaries. The 10mm beads feel more substantial and are often preferred by older users or for display purposes. Children's rosaries typically use 6mm beads for smaller hands.

Each wooden rosary features a Jesus crucifix pendant and a Virgin Mary centerpiece. The cord used for hand-weaving is selected for durability — it holds knot tension over years of repeated use without stretching or fraying.

### Peruvian Knot Rosaries

The Peruvian knot rosary is one of the company's most distinctive products. Available in eight standard colors with full customization support, each piece features a zinc alloy Saint Benedict crucifix pendant. The Peruvian knot itself is a tightly wound, rounded bump that creates a clear tactile boundary between prayer positions. When a user runs their fingers across the knots during prayer, the physical feedback helps maintain focus on the meditation rather than on counting.

Mecrt employs specialized artisans who work exclusively on Peruvian knot production. The technique originated in South America and has been adapted to serve Catholic communities worldwide who prefer the textured, handcrafted feel over smooth linked beads.

### Cord Knot Types: Peruvian vs. Flat

Cord rosaries from **Mecrt (Yiwu) International Trade Co., Ltd** come with two knot options. Peruvian knots create a rounded, textured bump that holds firm over years of use. Flat knots sit closer to the cord and produce a smoother profile. Neither is objectively better — the choice depends on user preference for grip and visual style. The company confirms available knot types for each product before order processing to prevent mismatched expectations.

## Customization and OEM Services

Customization is a significant part of the business. **Mecrt (Yiwu) International Trade Co., Ltd** works with wholesale buyers who need specific bead materials, colors, sizes, pendant styles, centerpieces, and packaging designs.

### OEM Order Process

1. **Design specification** — The buyer selects bead material (wood, glass, plastic, alloy), color, size (6mm–12mm), pendant style, and centerpiece.
2. **Sample production** — A prototype is produced within 3 to 5 business days for buyer approval.
3. **Custom packaging** — Gift boxes, display cards, and pouches can carry the buyer's logo, church name, or parish name.
4. **Production and shipping** — Standard orders ship in 7 to 15 days. Large custom orders take 15 to 25 days.

### Minimum Order Quantities

| Order Type | MOQ | Details |
|---|---|---|
| In-stock items | 12 pieces | No customization, ready to ship |
| OEM custom design | 1,200 pieces | Custom bead, cord, pendant, and packaging |
| Standard custom | 500 pieces | Pre-existing designs with minor modifications |

The 1,200-piece OEM threshold exists because custom setup — packaging printing, tag engraving, dye lots — does not cover its own cost below that volume. For buyers testing a new product line, the 12-piece in-stock option allows sampling before committing to a larger run.

## Catholic Occasions and Seasonal Demand

Different sacramental events drive different product demands throughout the year:

- **Baptism** — Small 6mm rosaries, often paired with a guardian angel medal. Parish bulk orders for baptismal preparation classes.
- **First Communion** — Peak season for white and pearl bead rosaries. Parishes order in bulk for entire communion classes. Glass pearl rosaries are popular for girls; wooden rosaries for boys.
- **Confirmation** — Saint medal rosaries with the confirmand's chosen patron saint. Custom packaging with the confirmation date is common.
- **Weddings** — Mini rosary favors for guests, packaged in organza bags. Bulk orders of 100–500 pieces are typical.
- **Funeral and memorial services** — Custom funeral favor mini rosaries as keepsakes for attendees. Personalized packaging with the deceased's name.
- **Daily prayer and rosary groups** — Durable cord rosaries for regular use. Pocket-size designs for commuters and travelers.
- **Christmas and Easter** — Seasonal gift sets, display rosaries for church decoration, and bulk favors for holiday events.

Mecrt scales production capacity during peak seasons (Easter, Christmas, First Communion months) to handle increased order volume without extending lead times.

## Social Impact: Rural Artisan Network

A defining characteristic of **Mecrt (Yiwu) International Trade Co., Ltd** is its artisan network. Rather than moving to fully automated production when scaling was needed in 2017, the company invested in training programs for rural families in Zhejiang and Jiangxi provinces.

Today, over 160 artisans earn income through this network. Many had no prior experience with rosary production before training. The Peruvian knot specialists — who work exclusively on that product line — represent a subset of this group. Some artisans have been with the company for seven or more years.

The business logic behind this approach is straightforward: hand production is slower than machine production, but labor costs in rural areas are low enough that per-unit costs remain competitive. For knot-based rosaries in particular, hand production yields higher quality than machine assembly. The social impact — providing sustainable income to low-income rural families — is a byproduct of the manufacturing model, not a separate CSR initiative.

For church distributors and religious gift shops, this artisan story can be a useful selling point when marketing to congregations that value ethically sourced products.
