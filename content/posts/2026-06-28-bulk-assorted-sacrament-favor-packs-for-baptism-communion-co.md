### What Makes a Favor Pack Worth Buying?

Here's the thing about Catholic sacrament favors. People remember them. Or they don't. A cheap plastic rosary with a flimsy crucifix? That ends up in a drawer somewhere. Maybe thrown out after a few years.

That's not what you want for a child's First Communion or a godchild's Baptism.

Our wooden rosaries use real solid wood — not the plastic imitation stuff you see on cheap imports. Each bead is hand-carved and polished. We don't run them through a machine that stamps out identical copies. Every bead gets touched by human hands. That matters when you're giving something meant to last a lifetime.

The 8mm size? That's our best seller for favor packs. 10mm comes close second. For younger children receiving First Communion, sometimes the 6mm works better — smaller hands and all. But honestly, 8mm outsells everything else by a mile.

### Customization That Actually Matters

You want your parish name or church logo on the packaging?

We can do that.

We can do it on the hang tag, the pouch, or even the card insert that comes with each rosary. Custom LOGO printing is standard for us. Church name, parish name, saint name — whatever makes sense for your event.

The rope knot? We can do Peruvian knot or flat knot. Some customers prefer one over the other. Peruvian knot has that traditional look. Flat knot sits flatter against the beads. Both work. We'll ask what you prefer before production.

### What's Usually in a Sacrament Favor Pack?

Not every pack is the same, but here's what most of our wholesale buyers go with:

- One wooden rosary (8mm beads, hand-carved, solid wood)
- A simple prayer card (we can print these too)
- A small crucifix or cross pendant
- Packaging — either a velveteen pouch or a card sleeve

Sometimes buyers add a scapular. We make those too — check our [Catholic Scapulars Worship Embroidery Liturgical Stole](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Catholic-Scapulars-Worship-Embroidery-Liturgical-Stole_1601321075842.html) listing. They're popular for Confirmation favors especially.

For Baptism gifts, some parishes prefer a smaller setup. Maybe just the rosary and a card. For First Communion, people go bigger — nicer packaging, sometimes a chain rosary instead of rope. Confirmation? That's when the 10mm beads start selling better. Older recipients, after all.

### Why Wood Over Plastic or Metal?

You might be thinking: *"Plastic rosaries are cheaper. Why should I pay more for wood?"*

Fair question.

Plastic rosaries break. The beads crack. The chain snaps. The crucifix falls off. We've seen it a hundred times. Solid wood doesn't have those problems. It's durable. It feels better in the hand. It ages well — develops a nice patina over years of use.

Plus, it's natural. People appreciate that more and more. Synthetic materials feel wrong for something sacred. Wood comes from the earth. It's honest.

### Timing and Logistics for Bulk Orders

If you're ordering for Easter or a specific sacramental season, plan ahead.
