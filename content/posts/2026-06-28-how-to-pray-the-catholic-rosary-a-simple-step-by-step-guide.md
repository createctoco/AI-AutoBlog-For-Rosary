### Step-by-Step: Praying the Rosary

Let’s walk through it. I’ll keep this practical.

#### 1. Start with the Crucifix

Hold the crucifix. Make the Sign of the Cross. Then pray the Apostles’ Creed.

“I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth…”

(You know this one, right? It’s the basic Christian creed. If you don’t have it memorized, a prayer card helps.)

#### 2. The First Three Beads

After the crucifix, you’ve got three small beads. These are for:

- First bead: Our Father
- Second bead: Hail Mary (for faith)
- Third bead: Hail Mary (for hope)
- Then a Glory Be

Why three? Tradition. The Church has been doing it this way for centuries. Don’t overthink it.

#### 3. Announce the First Mystery

This is where it gets personal. The rosary has four sets of mysteries: Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous. You pick one based on the day of the week.

- Monday & Saturday: Joyful Mysteries
- Tuesday & Friday: Sorrowful Mysteries
- Wednesday & Sunday: Glorious Mysteries
- Thursday: Luminous Mysteries

Each mystery has five “decades” (groups of ten Hail Marys). So you’ll do this five times total.

Announce the first mystery. Example: “The First Joyful Mystery: The Annunciation.” Then pray an Our Father.

#### 4. The Ten Beads (One Decade)

This is the core. Ten Hail Marys. One for each bead in the decade.

Here’s the rhythm: Say the Hail Mary while meditating on the mystery. Keep your mind on the event — the Annunciation, the Crucifixion, whatever you’re reflecting on. The beads keep your fingers busy so your heart stays focused.

After the tenth Hail Mary, pray a Glory Be.

Then the Fatima Prayer: “O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell…”

(Optional but recommended. Pope Leo XIII added it. Lots of Catholics use it.)

#### 5. Repeat for Four More Decades

Same pattern. Announce the mystery, Our Father, ten Hail Marys, Glory Be, Fatima Prayer. Five decades total.

By the end, you’ve prayed 50 Hail Marys. Plus the introductory prayers. It takes about 15-20 minutes if you’re focused. Some people stretch it to half an hour.

#### 6. Finish with the Hail Holy Queen

After the fifth decade, pray the “Hail, Holy Queen” (Salve Regina). Then a final Sign of the Cross.

Done.
