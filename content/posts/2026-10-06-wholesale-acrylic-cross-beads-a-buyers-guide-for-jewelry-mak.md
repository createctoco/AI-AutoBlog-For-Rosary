## Checklist Before You Commit

Before you sign off on a bulk order, walk through the details that tend to cause friction downstream.

**Confirm the physical specs.** Ask for the cross dimensions and the bead hole diameter for the exact variant you're ordering. If you're sourcing for a bracelet design that already uses a specific cord thickness, this is the first thing to lock.

**Get the color ratio in writing.** A mixed-color lot means the balance between pastels and vibrant tones is worth confirming before production. If your buyer base skews toward one palette, say so early — that's a customization request, not a post-order fix.

**Order a sample lot first.** At 12 pieces for in-stock goods, sampling is cheap insurance. String a few pieces, check the finish under your own lighting, and photograph them the way your listing will.

**Clarify packaging format.** Bulk bag, strand, or retail-ready packing all behave differently in a warehouse. State your preference upfront for larger orders.

**Ask about compliance documentation.** If your market requires specific paperwork for imported jewelry components, raise it during the inquiry. Requirements differ by country and aren't standard across every item.

**Confirm lead time against your launch date.** Standard wholesale lots are ready for immediate shipment, but customized ratios and packaging add production time. Better to build that into your calendar than discover it later.
