### What About 6mm or 12mm?

We get asked about 6mm rosaries sometimes. They’re small — almost too small for confirmation gifts, in our opinion. Kids lose them. Adults find them fiddly.

12mm beads? Those are chunky. Great for elderly folks with arthritis, but for a typical confirmation recipient? Too bulky.

Stick with 8mm or 10mm. That’s the sweet spot.

### The Knot Matters More Than You Think

Here’s something most buyers don’t consider until they get the order: the knot between beads.

We offer two options:

- **Peruvian knot** — tight, uniform, doesn’t slide around. Popular for daily use.
- **Flat knot** — a bit looser, gives the rosary some flexibility. Some people prefer the way it feels in hand.

Which one for confirmation? We recommend Peruvian knot. It holds up better over time. These rosaries get used — sometimes for decades. A good knot makes the difference between a rosary that lasts and one that falls apart.

### Custom Logo and Parish Branding

This is where we see a lot of confusion from first-time buyers.

“Can you put our church logo on the rosary itself?”

Short answer: no. Not directly on the beads. The beads are wood — you can’t stamp or engrave them without damaging the hand-polished finish.

What we *can* do:

- Custom hang tags with your church name or parish logo
- Custom packaging — boxes, pouches, cards
- Embossed or printed logo on the storage pouch or case

For confirmation, most parishes go with a custom card that includes the recipient’s name, confirmation date, and a short blessing. Simple but meaningful.
