## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before you place a bulk order, nail down the details that affect landed cost and sell-through.

- **Finish consistency.** Distressed finishes vary by nature. Ask how the aging treatment is controlled across a production run, and whether you'll approve a finish sample first.
- **Dimensions and weight.** These vary by item and affect both packaging and freight. Request the spec sheet rather than assuming from photos.
- **Wood type and construction.** Confirm the material and how the cross is assembled and finished.
- **Packaging.** Retail-ready packaging, bulk cartons, and private-label boxes are different cost lines. Confirm what's standard.
- **MOQ and pricing tiers.** Bulk pricing is quoted based on order quantity, target market, and branding needs, so there's no single published number. Ask for a tiered quote at a couple of volumes.
- **Sample coordination.** Samples are available for evaluation before volume commitment. Use that — a photo and a physical piece are not the same thing when finish is the selling point.
- **Shipping and destination requirements.** Confirm documentation and packing standards for your market early, since religious goods often move through mixed retail channels.
