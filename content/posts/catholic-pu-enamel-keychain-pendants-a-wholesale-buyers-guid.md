They end up in parish welcome packets, gift shop counter displays, fundraising tables, and confirmation or first-communion gift bags. Mecrt's Catholic metal PU micro-inlaid enamel color keychain is built for that middle ground.

## Specs and Build Quality

Quality problems in enamel keychains tend to come from the same three places regardless of supplier: enamel that chips at the edge, plating that dulls, and a split ring that opens under load. Mecrt's construction addresses the first two through the micro-inlay process — enamel is set into recessed areas of the metal rather than applied on top, which keeps the color contained and reduces the chance of flaking at the rim.

The metal core gives the piece its structural integrity; the polished finish is what a buyer notices first in a sample box. Enamel inlay resists fading and wear, so the religious motif stays legible over time. That's the manufacturer's claim, and you should verify it in your own sample — but the construction logic holds up.

The PU leather accent is a practical addition. It softens the look, keeps the piece from feeling like pure hardware, and gives the ring attachment a bit of give. The whole pendant is lightweight, so it won't pull a zipper or weigh down a bag strap.

What you won't find in the public description: exact dimensions, plating thickness, weight per piece, and metal composition. Those vary by motif and by item. Ask for a spec sheet when you inquire and compare it against your market's expectations. If you sell into the EU or North America, confirm plating and coating compliance for the specific finish you order — contact us to find out what documentation is available for your destination.

## Checklist Before You Commit

A sample answers more questions than a spec sheet does. Order one and handle it — the weight in your hand, the crispness of the enamel edge, whether the ring opens smoothly and closes tight.

Beyond the physical sample, work through these before signing off on a bulk order:

- **Motif accuracy.** Religious imagery has to be correct. Check the figure, the lettering, and the proportions against reference. Mistakes here don't show up in a photo — they show up in a customer complaint.
- **Color consistency across a batch.** Enamel lots can drift. Ask for a production sample from the same run, not just a reference piece.
- **Attachment strength.** Tug the ring and the PU accent. A keychain that fails at the attachment point is a return, not a sale.
- **Packaging.** Poly bag, backing card, or gift box? Each one changes landed cost meaningfully. Confirm what's included at the quoted price.
- **Compliance for your market.** Plating and coating rules differ by country. State your destination and ask what documentation is available.
- **Import obligations.** If you're bringing goods into the EU or US, know your duty and labeling requirements before the PO goes out.

Wondering whether your volumes qualify for a better tier? Ask directly. It's a shorter conversation than guessing.
