## Catholic Keychains — A Practical Product for the Faithful

You’d think a rosary is just a rosary. But then someone asks: “Can you make it smaller? Attach it to a key ring?” And that’s where Catholic keychains come in.

We’ve been making these for years. Church gift shops order them. Parishioners buy them for baptisms, First Communion, Confirmation, even weddings. A keychain rosary is not a compromise — it’s a different tool. One that fits in a pocket, hangs from a bag, or clips onto a car key.

Let me walk you through what we actually do.

---

### Real Wood, Real Hands

Here’s something we don’t advertise loudly, but I’ll say it straight: our keychain rosaries use solid natural wood. Not plastic. Not resin pretending to be wood. Real wood, hand-carved and polished one bead at a time.

The 8mm size is our bread and butter. Why? Because it’s big enough to feel substantial, small enough to fit on a keychain without looking like a weapon. 10mm works too, but for keychains, 8mm wins. We’ve tried 6mm — honestly, it’s too tiny for most adults. 12mm? That’s for full-size rosaries, not keychains.

The crucifix on these? Hand-assembled. The center medal? We can put a saint on it, or Our Lady, or just a simple cross. Customers pick.

---

### Knots That Hold

You might not think about the knots. But they matter.

We can do Peruvian knot — that’s the traditional style, tight and clean. Or flat knot, if you want something flatter against the beads. Both hold. Neither slips. We’ve had orders where the buyer specified “Peruvian knot only” — and we respect that.

For keychains, we usually attach a small metal ring. Some customers prefer a lobster clasp. Others just want a loop of cord. We’ll do whatever.

---

### Who Buys These?

Catholic keychains aren’t just for personal use. Think about the volume:

- Baptism gifts — small, meaningful, easy to mail.
- First Communion favors — parents buy in bulk.
- Confirmation keepsakes — parishes order 200 at a time.
- Wedding rosaries — some brides give them as bridesmaid gifts.
- Funeral memorials — a quiet way to remember.

We’ve seen orders from individual Catholics buying a dozen for their prayer group. We’ve seen churches ordering 500 for a retreat. And we’ve seen importers ordering 2400 for their catalog.

The [MOQ for OEM with your logo is 1200 pieces](https://mecrt.en.alibaba.com/). For stock items, you can start at just 12. Buy more, pay less. That’s how we work.

---

### Custom Logo? Yes.

Want your church name on the packaging? The parish name on a card? Your diocese crest on the back? We can do that.

We’ve printed on fabric pouches, cardboard tags, even direct on the center medal (if the quantity is high enough). It’s not complicated. You send us the artwork, we handle the rest.

One customer wanted “St. Joseph’s Parish” on every keychain. We did 1800 pieces. Another wanted a simple cross and no text. We did that too.
