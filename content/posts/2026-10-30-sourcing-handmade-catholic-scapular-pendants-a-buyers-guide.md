## What to Verify Before Ordering

Ask for a sample. One physical piece will answer questions about weight, finish, and clasp quality that no spec sheet covers, and it's the fastest way to judge whether the handmade variation sits inside your tolerance.

Then work through the practical list. Confirm chain length and clasp type for each variant you plan to stock. Confirm how the pendant is packaged and whether the packaging can carry your own labelling or barcode. Confirm the exact MOQ against your chosen configuration, since customization usually moves that number. Confirm what documentation your market requires — if you need paperwork for a particular destination, raise it now rather than at the port.

Wondering about lead times? Those depend on order size, customization, and season, so the sales team will quote against your actual requirement. Don't assume a standard window applies to a custom run in Q1.

It's also worth asking how the pieces are packed for transit and how many units go into a master carton. Small detail, but it changes your landed cost calculation.
