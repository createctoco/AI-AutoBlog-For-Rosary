## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Before placing a bulk order, a prudent buyer verifies several details that are not always clear from a product page. Based on the source material, here are the key questions to raise with Mecrt before committing to a volume.

**1. Exact dimensions and weight**
The product page describes the keychain as “compact” and “lightweight,” but does not list specific measurements or gram weight. For shipping cost calculations, display planning, and packaging design, you need precise numbers. Ask: *What is the total length (including clasp and ring)? What is the pendant width and thickness? What is the weight per unit with and without packaging?*

**2. Packaging options and unit cost**
The source material states that Mecrt supports custom packaging, but bulk buyers should confirm the standard packaging for stock orders (e.g., poly bag, card, or display box). Also, request tiered pricing for common order sizes (e.g., 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000 units). While the product page does not list specific pricing tiers, a direct inquiry will provide the information. *What is the unit price for stock orders at MOQ (12 pcs), and how does it decrease at higher quantities?*

**3. Lead time**
Production and shipping timelines vary by order type. Stock orders may ship quickly—possibly within days—while custom orders require design approval and tooling. Ask: *For a stock order of 500 pieces, what is the estimated lead time to dispatch? For an OEM order of 1,200 pieces with custom engraving, what is the typical production timeline?* The source material does not specify lead times, so this is essential.

**4. Sample availability**
Experienced buyers always request a pre-production sample before a large order. Mecrt’s product page does not mention sample policy, but most Chinese wholesale suppliers offer samples at cost plus shipping. Ask: *Can you provide a stock sample of the I Love Jesus Keychain? What is the sample cost and shipping method? For custom orders, is a custom sample available, and what are the charges?*

**5. Materials and finish certification**
Zinc alloy is common, but some markets (especially Europe and North America) require nickel-free or lead-free compliance for jewelry items. The source material does not mention certifications. Ask: *Does this keychain meet EU REACH or US California Proposition 65 requirements? Can you provide a material safety data sheet or test report?*

**6. Return and defect policy**
For wholesale orders, a small percentage of defective units is normal, but the supplier should have a clear policy. Ask: *What is your acceptable defect rate? How do you handle claims for damaged or incorrectly made items—replacement, credit, or discount on the next order?*
