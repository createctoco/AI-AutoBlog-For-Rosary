## What to Confirm with the Supplier

Before a bulk order moves, nail down the details that aren't obvious from a product photo. Internal dimensions are the first one — you need to know whether your rosary, bead strand, or keepsake actually fits, and whether there's clearance for a lining or insert. Ask about the metal substrate and the exterior finish, since that determines how the colorwork holds up under handling.

Then work through the mechanical and packaging questions:

- Lid or hinge type, and whether it stays closed in transit
- Interior treatment — bare metal, lining, or insert
- Iconography method and how consistent color reproduction is across a production run
- Individual packing (polybag, sleeve, or retail-ready) and master carton quantity
- Mixed-color ratios, and whether you can split a carton by colorway
- Sample availability, sample cost, and sample turnaround
- MOQ per color or per SKU, not just per order
- Production lead time, payment terms, and shipping method

Pricing, lead times, and carton specs aren't fixed across configurations, so treat any figure you see quoted generically as indicative only and ask for a written quotation tied to your exact specification. If you're ordering the OEM route at 1,200 pieces or more, request a pre-production sample and sign off on it in writing before the full run starts — that one step prevents most disputes later.
