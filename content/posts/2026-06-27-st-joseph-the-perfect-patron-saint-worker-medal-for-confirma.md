### The Practical Side — Sizes, Knots, and MOQs

Let's get into the nuts and bolts. Because you're probably ordering for a group, not just one person.

**Bead sizes:** Our wooden rosaries come in 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm. The 8mm is our best seller for confirmation gifts — small enough to fit in a pocket, big enough to feel substantial in the hand. 10mm works too if you want something more visible. 6mm we'd only recommend for very small hands or women's wrists. 12mm? That's for people who like a chunky rosary. Not everyone's thing.

**Knots:** We can do Peruvian knot or flat knot. Peruvian knot holds tighter over time — we recommend it for rosaries that get daily use. Flat knot looks cleaner, more modern. Your call.

**Custom logo:** Want your parish name or church name on the packaging? We can do that. Custom logo on the medal itself? That's a bigger order, but yes. We've done it for dioceses, retreat centers, and even a few cathedral gift shops.

### What About the Other Saints?

Don't get us wrong — we love St. Patrick, St. Francis, St. Michael. They all have their place. But for confirmation? For a gift that says "go out and do good work in the world"? St. Joseph hits different.

He's the saint of action. Of showing up. Of doing the job nobody else wanted to do (raising the Son of God? That's a big ask). He didn't say much in the Gospels — zero recorded words — but he did everything right.

That's a powerful message for a 14-year-old getting confirmed. Or a 30-year-old convert. Or anyone in between.
