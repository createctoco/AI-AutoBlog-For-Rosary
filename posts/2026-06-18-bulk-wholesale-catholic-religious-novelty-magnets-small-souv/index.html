What Makes a Good Religious Magnet?
#

Honestly, it’s the details. Not the magnet itself — that’s simple. But the artwork matters. The finishing matters. If the image looks blurry or the edges are rough, nobody buys it. We’ve seen cheap imports from overseas that look like a bad photocopy on a fridge. That’s not what we do.

Our magnets start with real Catholic art. Licensed imagery. Then we print it clean, laminate it, and mount it on a strong magnetic base. The whole thing is maybe 2 inches across. Fits in a pocket. Easy to ship.

We offer custom options too. Want your parish name or a specific saint on there? We can do that. Want a specific color border or a quote? Tell us. MOQ for custom magnets is lower than you’d think — 500 pieces. For stock designs, you can start at just 50 pieces. That’s nothing for a church group or a small distributor.
