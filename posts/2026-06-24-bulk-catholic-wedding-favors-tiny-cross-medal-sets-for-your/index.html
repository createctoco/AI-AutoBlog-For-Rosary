Catholic Wedding Favors: Tiny Cross and Medal Bulk Packs That Actually Make Sense
#

We get this question a lot from parish buyers and wedding planners. “Can you do a small cross or medal for our wedding favors? We need a lot, but we don’t want cheap junk.”

Honestly, we can. And we’ve been doing it for years.

Here’s the thing about Catholic wedding favors — they’re not just trinkets. They’re keepsakes. People hold onto them. They put them in a drawer, or on a nightstand, or pass them down. So you want something that feels real. Not plastic. Not something that breaks before the reception ends.

Our tiny cross and medal bulk packs are made with the same care as our rosaries. Solid materials. Real metal. No shortcuts.

What We Put in Those Bulk Packs
#

We don’t do one-size-fits-all. Some parishes want a simple cross pendant. Others want a medal of the Blessed Mother or a favorite saint. Some want both — a cross and a medal on one card.

Here’s what we’ve learned from years of B2B orders:

  • Tiny crosses — usually 1 inch to 1.5 inches. Sterling silver, gold tone, or antique bronze finish. We can do them with or without a chain.
  • Medals — Our Lady of Grace, St. Joseph, St. Michael, St. Benedict. You name it. We’ve got molds for most popular saints.
  • Bulk packaging — You can get them loose in bags, or carded with a ribbon. Custom packaging with your parish name or couple’s names? We do that too.

The 8mm size? That’s our best seller for rosaries. But for tiny crosses, we usually go smaller — about 1.2 inches. Big enough to read, small enough to wear comfortably.

Real Metal, Not That Plated Junk
#

Let me be direct. A lot of “Catholic wedding favors” sold online are zinc alloy with a thin layer of plating. They tarnish in six months. We don’t do that.

Our crosses and medals are made from brass or stainless steel. Then we plate them properly. Gold tone, silver tone, antique brass — the finish lasts. We’ve had church buyers tell us they still see their wedding favors at family homes ten years later.

Same goes for our wooden items. Our Rose Fragrance Wooden Bead 6mm Holy rosaries use real solid wood — not the plastic imitation stuff you see on cheap imports. Each bead is hand-carved and polished. If you want something natural and fragrant for your favor table, that’s a solid option.

One Thing People Forget: The Knot
#

You’d be surprised how many wedding favor crosses come with a chain that breaks. Or a cord that slips.

We use two types of cord for our crosses and medals:

  • Peruvian knot — tight, secure, looks clean. Good for larger medals.
  • Flat knot — traditional, sits flat against the neck. Popular for smaller crosses.

We can do either. Just tell us what your parishioners prefer.

We’re a rosary factory. But we do a ton of wedding favor orders. Why?

Because a Catholic wedding isn’t just a party. It’s a sacrament. People want something that reflects that. A tiny cross or a medal isn’t just a favor — it’s a reminder of the vows, the blessing, the community.

We’ve seen couples order 200 favors and then come back for their kids’ baptisms and first communions. That’s the kind of relationship we build.

If you’re looking for a Catholic Rosary Prayer Beads 8-10mm to go with your favors, we can bundle them too. Many parishes do that — give out a rosary at the wedding, then a cross at the baptism.

A Few Practical Tips
#

If you’re ordering wedding favors for the first time, here’s what we’ve seen work:

  1. Order samples first. Don’t guess. Get a sample of the cross and the card. Hold it in your hand.
  2. Think about the packaging. A card with a ribbon looks nicer than a bag. But bags are cheaper. Decide what fits your budget.
  3. Don’t forget the prayer card. Some parishes include a small prayer card with the favor. We can print those too.
  4. Check the metal. If you’re going with gold tone, make sure it matches your other decorations. Silver tone is more neutral.
