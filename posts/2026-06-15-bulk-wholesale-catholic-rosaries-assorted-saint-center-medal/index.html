Knots, Logos, and Customization

Cleaner look, easier to grip. Honestly, most wholesale buyers don’t care which one we use as long as it holds. But if you have a preference, we’ll match it.

Need your logo or church name on the packaging? Easy. We can customize the hang tags or the box itself. We’ve done this for parishes in the Philippines, Ghana, the US, and all over Europe.
